## Rule

Stilistica calls a plural used where it does not belong a grammatical error that is heard often lately. Its general rule: after a numeral, or after a group of words that indicates a quantity, the noun is not put in the plural form.

## Source

Stilistica di Papiamento (2008), §2 "Formulacion corecto", point 12 "Forma plural", PDF page 5.

## Correct

- cinco buki

## Exceptions

- When the definite article e or a possessive pronoun comes before the combination, the plural form is used (same page): RULE_STY_PLUR_002.

## Notes

- STI2008 has no printed page numbers; "5" is the PDF page.
- The page's other two examples are quantity phrases with set and variedad, followed by di and a singular noun. Only one example line is quoted because STI2008 is restricted. The page prints no incorrect form.
- Ruling (`_meta/invariants.md`): no -nan after a bare numeral; with an article or possessive it is fine.
- The same rule from Morfologia: RULE_GRA_PLURAL_001 and RULE_MOR_NOUN_PLURAL_002 (MOR2010 §2.3.1, p. 10).

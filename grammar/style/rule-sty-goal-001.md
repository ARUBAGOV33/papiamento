## Rule

Stilistica says that a sentence, spoken or written, should be correct, understandable, consistent and transparent (corecto, comprendibel, consecuente, transparente). It explains this as following the grammar, spelling and style rules.

## Source

Stilistica di Papiamento (2008), §2 "Formulacion corecto", the introduction before point 1, PDF page 2.

## Notes

- STI2008 has no printed page numbers; "2" is the PDF page.
- The page states an aim and prints no examples, so nothing is flagged by this rule. The numbered points of §2 have their own rules in this folder (some only describe a device and flag nothing): RULE_STY_REP_001 and RULE_STY_REP_002 (point 1), RULE_STY_TAUT_002 (2), RULE_STY_PLEO_001 (3), RULE_STI_ORDER_001 (6), RULE_STI_TRANSP_001 (7), RULE_STI_GER_001 (9), RULE_STY_ASYM_001 and RULE_STY_ASYM_002 (10), RULE_STI_CONC_001 (11), RULE_STY_PLUR_001 and RULE_STY_PLUR_002 (12).
- The old rule pack turned this into a style score with notes per dimension; the page gives no scoring method.
- STI2008 is restricted: the wording is restated, not quoted.

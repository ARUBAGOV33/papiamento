## Rule

Stilistica says that when the definite article e or a possessive pronoun stands before the combination of a numeral (or quantity phrase) and its noun, the plural form is used. Both of its examples have a numeral.

## Source

Stilistica di Papiamento (2008), §2 "Formulacion corecto", point 12 "Forma plural", PDF page 5.

## Correct

- e cinco bukinan

## Notes

- STI2008 has no printed page numbers; "5" is the PDF page.
- The page's second example has the possessive bo. The page then lists the possessive pronouns next to the personal ones: they have the same form, except that the possessive of e is su. Only one example line is quoted because STI2008 is restricted.
- This is the exception to RULE_STY_PLUR_001. It flags nothing: it only lifts that rule in these contexts.
- Ruling (`_meta/invariants.md`): no -nan after a bare numeral; with an article or possessive it is fine.
- Morfologia also uses nan after the definite article, a possessive or a demonstrative: RULE_MOR_NOUN_PLURAL_001 (MOR2010 §2.3.2, pp. 10–11).

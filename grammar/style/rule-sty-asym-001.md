## Rule

Stilistica calls it symmetry when a sentence that uses a pronoun such as nan keeps to it instead of switching later to another, such as bo. Asymmetry, for example starting with nos and ending with bo, is marked incorrect.

## Source

Stilistica di Papiamento (2008), §2 "Formulacion corecto", point 10 "Simetria/Asimetria", PDF page 4.

## Correct

- Nan a scohe pa nan ta nan so.

## Notes

- STI2008 has no printed page numbers; "4" is the PDF page.
- The page's incorrect example starts with nos, goes on with mi and addresses the reader as bo. No corrected version is printed, so nothing is listed under Incorrect, and the example is not quoted because STI2008 is restricted.
- The old rule pack's RULE_STY_ASYM_004 (keep person and number stable along a chain of references) restates this rule and is covered here.

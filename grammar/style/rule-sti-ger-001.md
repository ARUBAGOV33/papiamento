## Rule

Stilistica says the gerund shows that something is happening at that moment, and warns that sentences with a gerund easily lead to a grammatical error. When a gerund is used, its subject must be the same as the subject of the main clause.

## Source

Stilistica di Papiamento (2008), §2 "Formulacion corecto", point 9 "Frase cu gerundio", PDF page 4.

## Correct

- Cantando den bus e muchanan a bay Westpunt.

## Notes

- STI2008 has no printed page numbers; "4" is the PDF page.
- The page's incorrect example has the same gerund phrase, but its main clause makes e muchanan, who are the ones singing, the object of a hiba. The corrected line above makes e muchanan the subject. The page gives a second correct version that replaces the gerund with a clause introduced by mientras, with its own subject. Only one example line is quoted because STI2008 is restricted.
- The old rule pack had RULE_STY_GER_001, the same rule, which is covered here. RULE_STY_GER_002 and RULE_STY_GER_003 (rewrite any unclear gerund as a finite clause or with an explicit subject) go beyond the page, which offers the mientras clause only as one way to correct a wrong sentence.
- Gerund forms: RULE_MOR_GERUND_001 to RULE_MOR_GERUND_006 (Morfologia §6.6.2). por and prefera have no gerund (errata err-0010, err-0011); the gerund of drumi is drumiendo (err-0009).

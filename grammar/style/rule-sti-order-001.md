## Rule

Stilistica says to make sure that the order of the words in a sentence does not change its meaning in an unwanted way. In its example, to say that not all elderly people live near their children, no comes before tur.

## Source

Stilistica di Papiamento (2008), §2 "Formulacion corecto", point 6 "Secuencia di frase", PDF page 3.

## Correct

- No (ta) tur anciano ta biba cerca nan yiunan.

## Notes

- STI2008 has no printed page numbers; "3" is the PDF page.
- The page marks as incorrect the same sentence with tur first and no after the subject, an order that says no elderly person lives near their children. Only the corrected line is quoted because STI2008 is restricted; the parentheses around ta are printed on the page.
- A checker cannot know the intended meaning, so the pattern tur … no is at most a prompt to check the sentence.
- The old rule pack had the same point twice more: RULE_STY_SEQ_001 says the same thing and is covered here; RULE_STY_SEQ_002 adds semper and a general scope rule, which the page does not state.

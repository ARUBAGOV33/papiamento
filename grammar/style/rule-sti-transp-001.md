## Rule

Stilistica calls a sentence non-transparent when it is so long and complicated that the reader loses the thread of its content; such a sentence can become hard to read or understand, and the writer has to take great care not to make mistakes in formulating it. It says it is better to split the sentence into pieces.

## Source

Stilistica di Papiamento (2008), §2 "Formulacion corecto", point 7 "Frase no transparente", PDF page 3.

## Notes

- STI2008 has no printed page numbers; "3" is the PDF page.
- The page's example is one long sentence about a police patrol between Playa and San Nicolaas that fines the driver of a speeding car. The book says it is grammatically correct but stylistically poor because it has many unnecessary words, and asks the reader to reformulate it. No rewritten version is printed, so nothing is listed under Correct or Incorrect; the example is not quoted because it runs over three lines and STI2008 is restricted.
- The page gives no length limit and no score. The old rule pack's RULE_STY_TRANS_001 to RULE_STY_TRANS_004 (transparency scores, distance between subject and predicate, stacked modifiers) are not stated on the page and were not written. In the example two inserted clauses separate the subject Polis from its verb a duna, but the page draws no rule from that.

## Rule

After a colon, the first word of direct speech or of a complete sentence is written with a capital letter.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.2.1 Punto dobel, point 1, gazette page 29. "Ta skirbi cu mayusculo despues di punto dobel: e prome palabra di un estilo directo of un frase completo."

## Correct

- Su tanta a bisa: "Sigui ehempel di bo tata!"

## Notes

- §8.4 a (gazette page 25) says a colon is written before announced direct speech: Su mama a bis'e: "No lubida bo bukinan."
- Related: RULE_ORT_011 (lowercase after a colon), RULE_ORT_015 (independent sentence inside a sentence).

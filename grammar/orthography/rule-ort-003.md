## Rule

The letters a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h, i, j, k, l, m, n, ñ, o, p, q, r, s, t, u, v, w, x, y, z represent the phonemes of Papiamento. There are four digraphs: ch, dj, sh, zj.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §1 Alfabet, gazette page 6. "E siguiente letternan ta representa e fonemanan di Papiamento." "Tin cuatro digrafo: ch, dj, sh, zj"

## Notes

- The page prints the letters in a grid read column by column; the list above gives them in that order.
- Severity is info: the page describes the inventory. The only letter beyond the 26 Latin letters is ñ, and accented letters are handled by RULE_ORT_ACCENT_001.
- The digraphs are not split when dividing words into syllables (AB 2018 §6, gazette page 22).

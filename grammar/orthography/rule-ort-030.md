## Rule

When a verb that ends in a vowel is followed by the personal pronoun e and the vowel drops, it is always written with an apostrophe and without a space.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §10.1 "Uzo obligatorio di apostrof", point 1, gazette page 33. "Ta skirbi verbo cu ta caba cu vocal sigui pa e pronomber personal e semper cu apostrof y sin espacio di palabra, si e vocal cay afo."

## Correct

- Tecla a cumpr'e.
- E mucha a fang’e.
- Tinchi a sunch'e.
- Tinchi a bend’e.

## Notes

- The page sets each example beside a sentence with the full verb and a noun object instead of the pronoun e: Tecla a cumpra e buki., E mucha a fango e bala., Tinchi a sunchi Amanta., Tinchi a bende su auto. Those sentences are not incorrect forms.
- The section is headed "Uzo obligatorio di apostrof". §10.3 point 1 (gazette page 34) names this point as its exception: when the vowel drops before the pronoun e, the apostrophe is required, not optional. See RULE_ORT_034.
- Verb ending in i where the i becomes [j]: RULE_ORT_037. Verb ending in a consonant: RULE_ORT_038.
- The page prints both straight (') and curly (’) apostrophes; examples are copied as printed.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 33.

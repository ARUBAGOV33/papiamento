## Rule

A period is written at the end of a sentence. When a sentence ends with a part in parentheses, the period goes after the closing parenthesis; when a whole sentence is in parentheses, the period goes before the closing parenthesis.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §8.1 Punto (.), point a, gazette page 24. "Ta skirbi un punto: na final di un frase,"

## Correct

- Mañan mi no ta bay hunga bala.
- E ta biba na Rincon (Boneiro).
- E ta biba na Rincon. (Rincon ta keda na Boneiro.)

## Notes

- Points b and c of §8.1 on the same page are not covered by this rule: a period after abbreviations (p.e., dr.), except abbreviations written without a period internationally (km, g) and proper-name abbreviations written as the body or organization sets them (C.I.A., havo, UNESCO); and a period after an ordinal number (3.).

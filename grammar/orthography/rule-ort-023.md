## Rule

Names of holidays and of one or more religious days are written with capital letters, except for conjunctions and prepositions.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.3 Skirbimento di nomber, point i, gazette page 31. "nomber di dia di fiesta y un of mas dia religioso, cu excepcion di conhuncion y preposicion,"

## Correct

- Aña Nobo
- Asuncion
- Dera Gay
- Pasco
- Dia di Himno y Bandera
- Semana Santa

## Exceptions

- Conjunctions and prepositions inside the name stay lowercase (di and y in Dia di Himno y Bandera).

## Notes

- The names of the days of the week and of the months are lowercase (§9.6, gazette page 32; `_meta/invariants.md`).

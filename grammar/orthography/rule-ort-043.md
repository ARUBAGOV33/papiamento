## Rule

Numbers from 11 to 99 are written as one word, without an apostrophe and with i for the conjunction y; numbers above 100 are written as separate words, apart from their 11 to 99 part. A compound of a numeral and a noun is written as one word when a sound changes or drops. A numeral and or are written separately to tell the time.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.8 "Numeral", points 1 to 3, gazette page 38. "Combinacion di numeral di 11 te 99 ta skirbi hunto, sin apostrof y cu i pa conhuncion y."

## Correct

- 32 > trintidos
- 236 > dos shen y trintiseis
- of: dos shento y trintiseis (shent’y)
- of: dos cien y trintiseis
- 29.233 > bintinuebe mil dos shen y trintitres
- of: bintinuebe mil dos shento y trintitres (shent’y)
- of: bintinuebe mil dos cien y trintitres
- dosplaca, doria, treyotin
- 7 or, 12 or y mey, 11.30 or

## Exceptions

- Numeral + noun compounds: "Excepcion: paragraf 10.1, punto 3, pag. 33." The hour forms cuat'or, cinc'or, shet'or, och'or, nueb'or and cuant'or take an apostrophe; see RULE_ORT_032. Point 3 also refers to that paragraph.

## Notes

- The page's first example is printed "23 > bintiseis", but bintiseis is 26. Errata err-0001 (reviewed) and `_meta/invariants.md` decide that 23 is bintitres, so that example is left out above. ORT2009 has the same misprint (err-0002).
- `_meta/invariants.md` rests three rows on this page: 11 to 99 as one word, above 100 separately, and clock times with numeral and or apart.
- The old rule pack had this as a placeholder telling the checker to apply numeral rules before the general apostrophe and spacing rules. The page supports that order only through its cross-references: §10.2 (gazette page 33) names §11.8 as its exception (RULE_ORT_033), and §11.8 points 2 and 3 refer to §10.1 point 3. The rule above states what §11.8 itself says.
- Point 1b also refers to "10.2, pag. 33 y letter y-3, pag. 20".
- The page prints the three spellings of 236 and of 29.233 on separate lines, each alternative starting with "of:"; they are listed here one per line as printed.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 38.

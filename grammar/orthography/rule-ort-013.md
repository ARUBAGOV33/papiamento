## Rule

After an ellipsis (three dots) that stands inside a sentence and signals something unexpected or builds tension, the next word is written with a lowercase letter.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.2.2 Tres punto, gazette page 30 (the section starts on page 29). "Ta skirbi cu minusculo: despues di tres punto cu ta para dentro di un frase y cu ta indica algo inespera of cu ta pa hisa tension."

## Correct

- Diripiente ... un grito duro di pafo!

## Rule

tawata tin, tabata tin, tawata ta and tabata ta may be written as separate words, as one word, or with an apostrophe. When the last two syllables of tawata or tabata are dropped, an apostrophe is written.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §10.5 "Apostrof pa dos silaba cu ta cay afo", gazette page 34. "Ta skirbi e siguiente palabranan separa, hunto of cu apostrof."

## Correct

- tawata tin / tawatin / ta’tin
- tabata tin / tabatin / ta’tin
- tawata ta / tawata / ta’ta
- tabata ta / tabata / ta’ta
- (tawata ta > tawata) > ta’
- (tabata ta > tabata) > ta’

## Notes

- The page prints the three spellings in three columns; the slash above only separates them. All three are correct, so there is nothing to flag.
- The page prints the curly apostrophe (’) here; data files in this repo use the straight one (`_meta/conventions.md`).
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 34.

## Rule

For Dutch and English words written in their original form, a verb starts from the first person singular or the root. Derivations of verbs keep the original form, as do the derivations of hobby, kadastraal and parachute. Compounds also keep the original form.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §12.2 "Hulandes, Ingles y Frances", point 1, gazette page 42. "Den derivacion di verbo ta mantene e forma original (p.e. huurdo); tambe den derivacion di e palabranan: hobby, kadastraal, parachute."

## Correct

- tight (verb, from the first person singular or root)
- huurdo (derivation of a verb)
- hobby, kadastraal, parachute (their derivations keep this form)
- ijscream (compound)

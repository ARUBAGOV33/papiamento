## Rule

Geographic names are written with a capital letter, and so are the adjective, the name of the inhabitants and the name of the language that correspond to them.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.3 Skirbimento di nomber, point g, gazette page 31. "nomber geografico y adhetivo, nomber di habitante y nomber di idioma corespondiente,"

## Correct

- Francia
- un biña Frances
- un Frances
- Frances

## Notes

- The examples show the adjective capitalized too (un biña Frances).
- §9.3 e (gazette page 30) adds that every part of a geographic name is capitalized except conjunctions and prepositions (Sero di Compa, Sur America, Medio Oriente); the same point covers planets and astrological signs (Saturno, Venus, Acuario). No rule in this batch covers §9.3 e.
- Language names that are not derived from a geographic name: RULE_ORT_022. Days and months are lowercase (§9.6, gazette page 32; `_meta/invariants.md`).

## Rule

When the personal pronoun e follows a verb that ends in a consonant, the verb and e are written as separate words.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.1 "Skirbimento hunto of separa relaciona cu verbo", point b, gazette page 35. "Si e pronomber personal e ta para tras di un verbo cu ta caba cu consonante, ta skirbi esakinan separa."

## Correct

- Tinchi a dal e.

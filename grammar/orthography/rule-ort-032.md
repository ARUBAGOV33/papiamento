## Rule

The six words cuat'or, cinc'or, shet'or, och'or, nueb'or and cuant'or are written with an apostrophe.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §10.1 "Uzo obligatorio di apostrof", point 3, gazette page 33. "Ta skirbi e siguiente palabranan cu apostrof:"

## Correct

- cuat'or, cinc'or, shet'or
- och'or, nueb'or, cuant'or

## Notes

- The page gives the list without context. §11.8 (gazette page 38) links it to telling the time: a numeral and or are written separately (7 or, 12 or y mey), with a reference to this point, and point 2 there names it as the exception to joined numeral + noun compounds. See RULE_ORT_043.
- `_meta/invariants.md` says clock times are written as a numeral and or, separately; these six forms are the listed apostrophe forms.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 33.

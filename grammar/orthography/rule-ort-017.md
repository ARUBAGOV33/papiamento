## Rule

First names and family names, pet names (nomber di cariño) and nicknames (nomber di cherche) are written with a capital letter.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.3 Skirbimento di nomber, point a, gazette page 30. "Ta skirbi cu mayusculo: nomber y fam, nomber di cariño y nomber di cherche,"

## Correct

- Maria Tromp
- Linchi
- Cha Tiger

## Notes

- The rule pack's title said "search names"; nomber di cherche is a nickname, not a search name.
- §9.3 c on the same page (proper names of animals: "Nos cacho Bufi no ta gusta pisca.") is not covered by any rule in this batch.
- Related: RULE_ORT_018 (appellatives with a name).

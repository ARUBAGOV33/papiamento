## Rule

Titles of literary, musical, visual-art, visual and audiovisual works are written with a capital letter. A title may also be written the way the creator of the work has set it.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.3 Skirbimento di nomber, point l, gazette page 31. "titulo di obra literario, musical, plastico, visual, audiovisual etc.," "Nota: Por skirbi e titulo tambe segun e creador di e obra a stipula."

## Correct

- Cetilalma
- Arubanita
- Bestial

## Exceptions

- The creator's own spelling of the title is also allowed (the Nota).

## Notes

- Severity is warning: because the creator may set the spelling, a checker cannot be sure a different capitalization is wrong.
- The examples are one word each, so the page does not show whether every word of a longer title is capitalized.
- §9.3 k (san, santa, santo before a person's name: San Pedro, Santa Teresa, Santo Domingo) and §9.3 m (La Birgen, La Reina) on the same page are not covered by any rule in this batch.

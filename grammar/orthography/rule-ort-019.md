## Rule

Names of deities, pronouns that refer to a deity, and names of prayers and of holy scriptures are written with a capital letter; the names of holy scriptures only when they refer to the content.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.3 Skirbimento di nomber, point d, gazette page 30. "nomber di deidad, pronomber cu ta referi na deidad, nomber di oracion y di scritura santo (ora e ultimo aki ta referi na e contenido),"

## Correct

- Ta E ta nos Señor, nos Dios.
- Laga Bo reino bini na nos.
- Ave Maria
- Nos Tata
- Bijbel
- Koran

## Notes

- A capital Bo can also be a courtesy form (§9.5, gazette page 32), which is a different rule.
- §9.3 j (gazette page 31) prints Dios with a capital in "Tanto e hudiunan como e catoliconan ta kere den Dios."

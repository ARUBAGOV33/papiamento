## Rule

When an apostrophe is used, the two words are written without a space between them. Only with the negation no is the space kept.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §10 "Apostrof" (opening paragraph), gazette page 33. "Ora uza un apostrof, ta skirbi e dos palabranan sin espacio di palabra."

## Correct

- Tecla a cumpr'e.
- Tinchi a bend’e.
- E bolo ta p’e.

## Exceptions

- The negation no keeps the space: Mi n’ sa. (page 33). The page also prints N' ta berdad? (§10.2, page 33) and Nos n' come. (§10.4, gazette page 34).

## Notes

- The correct examples come from §10.1 on the same page.
- The same no-space condition is repeated in §10.1 point 1 (RULE_ORT_030) and in the heading of §10.3 (RULE_ORT_034, RULE_ORT_035).
- The page prints both straight (') and curly (’) apostrophes; examples are copied as printed.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 33.

## Rule

A word whose final sound is pronounced as [o] or as [u] is written with o only.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §2 Vocal, gazette page 6. "Palabra cu na final tin un zonido cu ta pronuncia como [o] of como [u], ta skirbi solamente cu ‘o’."

## Correct

- duro
- puro

## Notes

- The same page lists sucu under the letter u (sound [u]), and AB 2018 §3.2 (gazette page 9) writes leu, pareu, riu, friu with a final u. So the rule is about a final sound heard as either [o] or [u], not about every final u. A checker must not change a final u to o on its own; it needs a word list.
- The same page also says muhe is written that way although it may be pronounced otherwise. That point is not covered here.

## Rule

The orthography tries to keep one written form per word. Although many words vary in pronunciation, it tried to keep no more than two variants, and it avoided more than one written form for a word as far as possible.

## Source

Ortografia di Papiamento (version april 2009), Introduccion, printed page 2. "A trata di mantene un forma den skirbimento di palabra." "Ehempel di dos opcion ta: abou / abao, grandesa / grandeza."

## Correct

- abou / abao
- grandesa / grandeza

## Notes

- Severity is info: the page states the principle. Which words have two spellings is set word by word in the annex and the word list, e.g. AB 2018 gazette page 9 (trabou / trabao, bou / bao, cuidou / cuidao), page 41 (naturalesa / naturaleza, alabansa / alabanza) and page 43 (blade/blet, leak / lik). A checker should accept both spellings of a listed pair.
- The AB 2018 gazette copy of the annex does not include the Introduccion, so ORT2009 is cited.

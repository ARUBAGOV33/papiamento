## Rule

A semicolon marks a pause shorter than a period and longer than a comma. It separates main clauses from each other, and it may be used between words or word groups that form part of an enumeration.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §8.2 Punto coma (;), gazette page 24. "Punto coma ta indica un pauze mas cortico cu punto y mas largo cu coma."

## Correct

- Su sapato a kibra; p’esey e no por a bay fiesta.
- Lo kita coriente den e siguiente barionan: Santa Helena, un parti; Pos Abou, un parti; Tanki Flip.

## Notes

- Severity is suggestion: the page says a semicolon "may" be used in an enumeration ("Por uza punto coma"), and whether two main clauses are better split by a semicolon or a period is a matter of judgement. A checker should only suggest it.
- Related: RULE_ORT_007 (period); §8.3 Coma (gazette page 25).

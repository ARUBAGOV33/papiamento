## Rule

The preposition pa followed by the personal pronoun e is always written with an apostrophe, as p’e.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §10.1 "Uzo obligatorio di apostrof", point 2, gazette page 33. "Ta skirbi preposicion pa sigui pa pronomber personal e semper cu apostrof (p’e)."

## Correct

- E bolo ta p’e.
- Deporte pa bon p’e.

## Notes

- The page prints the curly apostrophe here; data files in this repo use the straight one (`_meta/conventions.md`).
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 33.

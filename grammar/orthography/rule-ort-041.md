## Rule

A compound made of a verb and a verb is written as one word.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.1 "Skirbimento hunto of separa relaciona cu verbo", point e, gazette page 35. "Ta skirbi composicion cu ta consisti di verbo y verbo hunto."

## Correct

- cendepaga
- chocamata
- dalbay (sort’i pan)
- kiermen

## Notes

- "(sort’i pan)" is the page's own gloss: dalbay here is a kind of bread.
- The page gives four examples and does not call them a complete list; a checker can only apply this rule to known compounds.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 35.

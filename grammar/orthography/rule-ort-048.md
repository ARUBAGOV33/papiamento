## Rule

A reduplicated form is written as two separate words when it has two main stresses. pocopoco is always written as one word.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.10 "Reduplicacion", point b, gazette page 39. "Ta skirbi composicion di forma reduplica separa, si tin dos entonacion principal."

## Correct

- blanco blanco
- coti coti
- fini fini
- nobo nobo

## Exceptions

- "Ta skirbi e palabra ‘pocopoco’ semper hunto."

## Notes

- With one main stress the form is written as one word: RULE_ORT_047.
- A checker cannot hear stress, so it can only apply this rule to known forms such as those listed.
- The old rule pack also had RULE_ORT_REDUP_001 for the whole of §11.10; it was merged into this rule and RULE_ORT_047.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 39.

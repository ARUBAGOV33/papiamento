## Rule

Names of days and months are written with a lowercase letter.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.6 "Dia y luna", gazette page 32. "Ta skirbi nomber di dia y di luna cu minusculo:"

## Correct

- diadomingo, dialuna, diamars, diaranson, diahuebs, diabierna, diasabra
- januari, februari, maart, april, mei, juni, juli, augustus, september, october, november, december

## Notes

- The page does not say what happens at the start of a sentence. §9.1 (gazette page 29) writes the first word of every sentence with a capital.
- These are the day and month names in `_meta/invariants.md` (Ruling; AB §9.6). Spanish day and month names are barbarismos: `lexicon/barbarismos/barbarismos.tsv`, rows bar-0012 to bar-0033.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 32.

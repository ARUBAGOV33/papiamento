## Rule

The listed words from Spanish are written with a final z, and the z is kept in the plural and in derivations with the suffix -mente.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §12.1 Spaño, exception 2, gazette page 41. "Ta skirbi e siguiente palabranan cu letter z na final."

## Notes

- Severity: writing a listed z-word with a final s is an error.
- Nouns from Spanish -eza/-anza (s or z both correct) are covered by RULE_ORT_051.
- The page hyphenates several words of the z list across lines (anti-faz, dis-fraz, inca-paz, inma-durez, ridi-culez, timi-dez); they are listed above unbroken.
- lus is not a Papiamento word, so flagging it is right: the noun is luz (light) and the verb is lusa (to light), maintainer decision on 2026-10-19.
- The z list is not exhaustive (footnote: "E palabranan no necesariamente ta un cantidad absoluto.").
- The general rule of §12.1 (words from Spanish follow the Ortografia di Papiamento) and its exception list 1 are not covered here. The letter z entry (p. 20) points to this section.
- The listed words are in `lexicon/loanwords/loanwords.tsv` (e.g. loan-0065 cruz).
- Related: RULE_ORT_002 (one written form, a few accepted doublets).
- The rule pack cited ORT2009 p. 42, which is the PDF page; the printed page in both ORT2009 and AB 2018 is 41.

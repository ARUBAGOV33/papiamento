## Rule

After a colon, the first word of an announced part of a sentence, or of an announced enumeration, is written with a lowercase letter.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.2.1 Punto dobel, point 2, gazette page 29. "Ta skirbi cu minusculo despues di punto dobel:"

## Correct

- AWG. 25,-- skirbi na palabra: binticinco florin.
- E ta cumpra: keshi, pan y ham.

## Notes

- §8.4 b (gazette page 25) says a colon is written before an announced enumeration: Nan ta bende: mesa, cashi y cama.
- The page does not mention names, but words that §9.3 requires to be capitalized keep their capital after a colon.

## Rule

A noun from Spanish that ends in eza or anza in Spanish may be written with s or with z. This applies only when the part before eza or anza is spelled in Spanish the same way as in the Papiamento orthography. andansa and lansa are exceptions.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §12.1 "Spaño", point 3, gazette page 41. "Sustantivo cu ta bin di Spaño y cu na Spaño ta skirbi cu eza of anza na final por skirbi cu letter s of cu letter z."

## Correct

- naturalesa / naturaleza
- alabansa / alabanza

## Exceptions

- "Excepcion: andansa, lansa". The page prints them with s only and gives no z form.

## Notes

- Both spellings are correct, so there is nothing to flag for the words this point covers.
- The general rule for Spanish words is RULE_ORT_050. RULE_ORT_S_Z_001 from the old rule pack also covers this point.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 41.

## Rule

When a sound at the start of a word drops, or a sound at the end of a word drops, the word may be written in full or with an apostrophe. Both spellings are correct.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §10.2 "Apostrof pa zonido na cuminsamento y na final di palabra", gazette page 33. "Ta skirbi e palabra completo of cu apostrof:"

## Correct

- mashin di cose / mashin'i cose
- Mi no sa. / Mi n' sa.
- No ta berdad? / N' ta berdad?

## Exceptions

- "Excepcion: Mira 11.8, pag. 38." Numerals from 11 to 99 are written as one word without an apostrophe; see RULE_ORT_043.

## Notes

- The page prints each pair in two columns, full form and apostrophe form; the slash above only separates them.
- Nothing to flag: both forms are correct. When the apostrophe stands for the negation no, the space is kept (Mi n' sa.); see RULE_ORT_029.
- §10.4 (gazette page 34) gives a similar choice when a final vowel and a one-sound word both drop: Nos n' come. / Nos no a come. No rule in this batch covers §10.4.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 33.

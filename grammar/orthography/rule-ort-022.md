## Rule

Names of languages, and their adjectives, that are not derived from a geographic name are written with a capital letter.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.3 Skirbimento di nomber, point h, gazette page 31. "nomber di idioma y nan adhetivo, cu no ta deriva di nomber geografico,"

## Correct

- Papiamento
- Sranan Tongo

## Notes

- Ruling (`_meta/invariants.md`): the language name is Papiamento, with a capital P and ending in -o.
- Language names derived from a geographic name: RULE_ORT_021.

## Rule

A compound of a reduplicated basic word, or of another reduplicated form, is written as one word when it has only one main stress.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.10 "Reduplicacion", point a, gazette page 39. "Ta skirbi composicion di palabra basico reduplica y otro forma reduplica hunto, si tin solamente un entonacion principal."

## Correct

- lapilapi, melemele, pegapega, zuruzuru
- badabada, cachicachi, flengueflengue, shirishiri

## Notes

- With two main stresses the form is written as two words: RULE_ORT_048. pocopoco is always one word (the exception to point b).
- A checker cannot hear stress, so it can only apply this rule to known forms such as those listed.
- The old rule pack also had RULE_ORT_REDUP_001 for the whole of §11.10; it was merged into this rule and RULE_ORT_048.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 39.

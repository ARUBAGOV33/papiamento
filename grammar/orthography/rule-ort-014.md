## Rule

The first letter of the first word of a sentence is a capital.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.1 Mayusculo na cuminsamento di frase, point a, gazette page 29. "Ta skirbi cu mayusculo: e prome letter di e prome palabra di un frase,"

## Correct

- Tur siman e ta come un webo herbi.
- Uzo: Mescla cada liter cu mey liter di awa friu.

## Notes

- The second example also shows a capital after a colon that introduces a complete sentence; see RULE_ORT_010.
- Related: RULE_ORT_015 (independent sentence inside a sentence), RULE_ORT_016 (letters).

## Rule

A word that refers to a dignity is written with a capital letter when the person's name follows it or the person is mentioned in the text. Appellatives that refer to a dignity, and their abbreviations, are also written with a capital. Titles and their abbreviations are written the way the educational institution set them.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.4 "Nomber di dignidad y titulo", gazette page 32. "Ta skirbi cu mayusculo:" … "apelativo cu ta referi na dignidad y nan abreviacion."

## Correct

- Rey Juan Carlos
- President Kennedy
- Su Altesa Real (S.A.R.)
- Su Excelencia (S.E.)
- Su Santidad (S.S.)
- doctor — dr.
- Bachelor of Arts — B.A.
- meester — mr.
- Master of Arts — M.A.
- doctorandus — drs.
- Licenciado — Lic.

## Notes

- The title list (point b) is printed in two columns, title and abbreviation; the dash above only separates them. Titles follow the institution's own spelling, so doctor, dr., meester and mr. are printed in lowercase.
- §9.5 "Forma di cortesia" on the same page allows Bo and Boso with a capital as a courtesy form. No rule in this batch covers it.
- Related: appellatives used with a name (§9.3, gazette page 30) are covered by RULE_ORT_018.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 32.

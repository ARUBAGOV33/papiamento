## Rule

After a comma, the next word is always written with a lowercase letter. The one exception is the word after the comma that follows the greeting at the start of a letter.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.2.3 Coma, gazette page 30. "Despues di coma semper ta skirbi minusculo."

## Correct

- "Di unda bo ta bin?”, su ruman a puntr'e.
- "Stop di zundra, homber ...!", el a grit'e.

## Exceptions

- The page refers to §9.1 c (gazette page 29): the word after the comma that follows the greeting at the start of a letter is capitalized. See RULE_ORT_016.

## Notes

- The page does not mention names, but words that §9.3 (gazette pages 30–31) requires to be capitalized, such as personal names, keep their capital after a comma. A checker must not lowercase them.
- The first example has a straight opening quote and a curly closing quote, as printed.

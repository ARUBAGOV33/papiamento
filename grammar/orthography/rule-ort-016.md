## Rule

At the start of a letter, the word after the comma that follows the greeting is written with a capital letter.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.1 Mayusculo na cuminsamento di frase, point c, gazette page 29. "e palabra despues di e coma cu ta aparece tras di e saludo na cuminsamento di un carta."

## Correct

- Apreciabel Señor Wernet,
- Mi ta gradici Bo ...

## Notes

- The example is printed as two lines: the greeting, then the first line of the letter.
- This is the exception to RULE_ORT_009 (lowercase after a comma); §9.2.3 (gazette page 30) refers back to this point.
- §8.3 f (gazette page 25) puts a comma after the greeting and after the closing formula before the signature, e.g. Atentamente,
- Bo in the example has a capital; §9.5 (gazette page 32) allows bo and boso with a capital as a courtesy form. That is a separate point.

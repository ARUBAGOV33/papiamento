## Rule

Names of peoples and tribes, and their derivations, are written with a capital letter. When the word refers to a religion or a race, no capital is used.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.3 Skirbimento di nomber, point j, gazette page 31. "nomber di pueblo y tribu y e derivacionnan," "Nota: Den caso cu ta referi na religion of rasa no ta uza mayusculo."

## Correct

- Indjan
- Caquetio
- Azteca
- Hudiu
- Tanto e hudiunan como e catoliconan ta kere den Dios.
- Na Aruba tin hopi hende cu rasgo indjan.

## Notes

- The last two examples are the Nota's lowercase cases (religion, race).
- Severity is warning: the same word can name a people or refer to a religion or race, so the choice depends on meaning and a checker should flag the case for review rather than correct it.

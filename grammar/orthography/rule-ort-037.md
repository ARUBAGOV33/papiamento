## Rule

When the personal pronoun e is the dative or accusative object after a verb that ends in i, and that i changes to [j], the verb and e are written as one word, following the diphthong rules of §3.1.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.1 "Skirbimento hunto of separa relaciona cu verbo", point a, gazette page 35. "ta skirbi nan hunto teniendo cuenta cu e reglanan tocante diptongo den paragraf 3.1."

## Correct

- Su mama a bistie.
- E cas di su wela, ta nan a construye.

## Notes

## Rule

A rising diphthong is written with y or w when it stands at the beginning of a syllable. It is written with i or u when it does not stand at the beginning of a syllable.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §3.1 Diptongo creciente, gazette page 7. "Ta skirbi diptongo creciente cu y of w ora nan ta para na cuminsamento di silaba." "Ta skirbi diptongo creciente cu i of u ora nan no ta para na cuminsamento di silaba."

## Correct

- yabi, cabuya
- yen, cayente
- yerbe
- beyisima
- yora, desaroyo
- yorki
- yuda, desayuno
- wanta, warawara
- weso, caweta
- welder
- wiri, winchi
- wowo, awor
- wordo
- rabia, papia
- miedo, biento
- spierto
- cambio, propio
- ciudad, calcium
- igual, cuatro
- pueblo, berguensa
- cuerde
- cuido, atribui
- continuo, antiguo

## Exceptions

- kwihi, twil, twis (p. 7).
- The page also sends the reader to the letter y (gazette page 20) and the letter w (gazette page 18). There: diahuebs, Huebs Santo, huerfano are written with hu rather than w (p. 18); when [u] or [o] comes before the sound [w], no w is written, e.g. hangua, ruina, heroe, poesia, except deheluwe, wowo, yuwana (p. 18); when [e] or [i] comes before [ja], [jo] or [ju], no y is written, e.g. deseo, pelea, sea, dia, simpatia, pedagogia, except a list that includes biyar, biyon, briya, maraviya (p. 20).

## Notes

- The cross-reference on p. 7 reads "letter y-1 punto b, pag. 20", but on p. 20 the exceptions are under the letter y, point 2.
- Triphthongs follow the same pattern: y or w at the start of a syllable, i or u elsewhere (§4, gazette page 9).

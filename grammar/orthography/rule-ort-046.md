## Rule

A combination of bon with a basic word is written as separate words, except bonbida and bonbini.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.9 point 2 "Uzo di ’bon’", gazette page 39. "Combinacion di ‘bon’ cu palabra basico ta skirbi separa."

## Correct

- bon dia
- bon nochi
- bon mucha
- bon ehempel

## Exceptions

- "Excepcion: bonbida, bonbini." Both are written as one word.

## Rule

Apart from the verbs listed in §11.9 point 1a, a combination of mal or mala with a basic word is written as separate words; malagradecido, malcontento and malora are the exceptions. A word that is not a combination of mal with a basic word is written as one word, and so are its derivations.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.9 "Uzo di ’mal/mala’ y ’bon’", points 1b and 1c, gazette page 39. "Den otro caso ta skirbi combinacion di ‘mal’ of ‘mala’ cu palabra basico separa."

## Correct

- mal para, mal fashi, mal fama
- mal(a) rabia, mal(a) mucha, mal(a) maña
- maldito, maldicion, malhechor, maldad, malvado (point 1c)

## Exceptions

- Written as one word: malagradecido, malcontento, malora (mala suerte)

## Notes

- The old rule pack named "mal uso" here; the page prints mal uzo, and it is the exception to point 1a on page 38, not to this point. See RULE_ORT_044.
- The page underlines "cu palabra basico" (with a basic word) in point 1b.
- The old rule pack also had RULE_ORT_MAL_001 for §11.9; it was merged into this rule and RULE_ORT_044.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 39.

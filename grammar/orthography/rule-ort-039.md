## Rule

The personal pronoun e is written el before the auxiliary verb a when an [l] sound is heard in pronunciation. el and a are written as separate words.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.1 "Skirbimento hunto of separa relaciona cu verbo", point c, gazette page 35. "Ta skirbi ‘el’ y ‘a’ separa."

## Correct

- El a bay caba.

## Notes

- Whether to write e or el depends on pronunciation, which a checker cannot hear. Only el and a written together can be flagged without doubt.
- The old rule pack had this as a warning; the page states both parts as rules ("Ta skirbi"), so the severity here is error.
- The page misprints "pronunciacion" as "pronuniciacion" in this point; that word is not quoted above.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 35.

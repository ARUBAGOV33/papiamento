## Rule

When a word ends in a vowel that drops and the next word starts with a vowel or is a single vowel, the words are written either separately and in full, or with an apostrophe and no space.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §10.3 "Skirbimento cu apostrof y sin espacio di palabra", point 1, gazette page 34. "Ta skirbi separa y e palabra completo of cu apostrof y sin espacio di palabra:"

## Correct

- ta asina > t'asina
- mi a bay > m'a bay
- Tinchi ta cumpra e auto > Tinchi ta cumpr’e auto.
- Tecla ta cumpra e auto aki > Tecla ta cumpr’e auto aki.

## Exceptions

- "Excepcion: Mira paragraf 10.1-1, pag. 33." When a vowel-final verb is followed by the personal pronoun e and the vowel drops, the apostrophe is obligatory, not optional; see RULE_ORT_030.

## Notes

- Both forms are correct. What can be flagged is a space next to the apostrophe in the contracted form; the page's heading says "sin espacio di palabra", as does §10 (RULE_ORT_029).
- In the last two examples e is followed by a noun (e auto), unlike the pronoun e of §10.1 point 1.
- The page prints a double space in "Tinchi  ta cumpr’e auto."; it is copied here with a single space. Apostrophes are copied as printed (straight and curly).
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 34.

## Rule

The pronouns mi, bo, nos, boso and nan are always written as separate words when they come after a verb.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.1 "Skirbimento hunto of separa relaciona cu verbo", point d, gazette page 35. "Si e pronombernan mi, bo, nos, boso, nan ta para tras di un verbo, ta skirbi nan semper separa."

## Correct

- bisti mi
- bisti bo
- bisti nos
- bisti boso
- bisti nan

## Notes

- §11.2 point b (same page): when two personal pronouns follow each other they are written separately, in some cases also with an apostrophe, e.g. "El a bisti mi e. (of: bisti m'e)". No rule in this batch covers §11.2.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 35.

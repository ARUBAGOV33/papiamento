## Rule

An apostrophe shows that one or more sounds, written as one or more letters, have dropped out.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §10 "Apostrof" (opening paragraph), gazette page 33. "Ta uza un apostrof pa indica cu un of mas zonido (representa pa un of mas letter) a cay afo."

## Correct

- Tecla a cumpr'e.
- E bolo ta p’e.
- cuat'or
- mashin'i cose
- Mi n' sa.

## Notes

- The examples come from §10.1 and §10.2 on the same page; the opening paragraph itself only gives Mi n’ sa.
- Severity is info, not the old pack's error: this paragraph says what the apostrophe is for, not where it must be written. The same pages print correct forms where sounds drop without an apostrophe (paticabra and tawatin on page 34, trintidos and dosplaca on page 38), so a missing apostrophe can only be flagged under the obligatory cases below.
- Where the apostrophe is obligatory: RULE_ORT_030, RULE_ORT_031, RULE_ORT_032 (§10.1). Where it is optional: RULE_ORT_033 (§10.2), RULE_ORT_034 and RULE_ORT_035 (§10.3), RULE_ORT_036 (§10.5). Spacing at the apostrophe: RULE_ORT_029.
- The page prints both straight (') and curly (’) apostrophes; examples are copied as printed. Data files in this repo use the straight apostrophe (`_meta/conventions.md`).
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 33.

## Rule

Names of countries, regions, parts of countries or continents that are made of more than one word, and the words derived from them, are written as separate words. The page lists five derived words as exceptions.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.11 "Skirbimento di nomber geografico", gazette page 40. "Nomber di pais, region of parti di pais of continente, cu ta consisti di combinacion di palabra, y nan derivacionnan ta skirbi separa."

## Correct

- Sur Africa
- Sur Africano
- Medio Oriente

## Exceptions

- Costaricense, Estadounidense, Portoriqueño, Costariqueño, Islaribeño

## Notes

- §12.2 exception b (gazette page 42): geographic names are written without a hyphen, e.g. Noord Korea. See RULE_ORT_052.
- Capital letters for geographic names and their derivations: §9.3 (gazette page 31), covered by RULE_ORT_021.
- Costariqueño and Portoriqueño are also on the §12.1 list of words from Spanish (gazette page 41; RULE_ORT_050).
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 40.

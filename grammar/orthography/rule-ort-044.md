## Rule

The listed verbs formed with mal are written as one word, and so are their derivations and their use as adjectives. mal uzo is the exception and is written as two words.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.9 "Uzo di ’mal/mala’ y ’bon’", point 1a, gazette page 38. "Ta skirbi e siguiente verbonan como un palabra. Tambe e derivacionnan y uzo como adhetivo ta skirbi hunto."

## Correct

- maluza, malgasta, maltrata, malcria
- malogra, malinterpreta, maldiciona, malversa
- malcriansa, malgastamento (derivations)
- un mucha malcria (adjective use)

## Exceptions

- "Excepcion: mal uzo ta skirbi separa." (page 38)

## Notes

- Footnote 1 (page 38): the list of verbs is not necessarily complete, "E palabranan no necesariamente ta un cantidad absoluto."
- Remarca (page 39): not every case is a derivation of these verbs. The page compares "No ta maltrata bestia." and "Maltrato di bestia ta condenabel." with "Nan a trata nos malo." and "Nos a ricibi un mal trato."
- Other combinations with mal or mala: RULE_ORT_045. §11.1 point f (gazette page 35) refers to this point; see RULE_ORT_042.
- The old rule pack also had RULE_ORT_MAL_001 for §11.9; it was merged into this rule and RULE_ORT_045.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on pages 38-39.

## Rule

When the sound at the end of a word, or a schwa inside it, drops and at the same moment the first sound of the next word also drops, the words may be written separately and in full, or with an apostrophe and no space. paticabra is listed as an exception.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §10.3 "Skirbimento cu apostrof y sin espacio di palabra", point 2, gazette page 34. "si e zonido na fin di un palabra of un shua den un palabra cay afo y na mesun momento e zonido na cuminsamento di e siguiente palabra tambe cay afo."

## Correct

- weso di lomba / wes'i lomba
- sopi di carni / sop'i carni
- boter di lechi / botr'i lechi

## Exceptions

- "Excepcion: paticabra". The page prints it as one word without an apostrophe.

## Notes

- "shua" is the schwa [ə]: compare §5, letter g (gazette page 13), "Ora ta sigui un shua tras di e zonido [g]", with binager and sanger. In boter di lechi > botr'i lechi the schwa of boter drops.
- The page prints each pair in two columns; the slash above only separates them.
- Both spellings are correct, so the choice itself is not flagged. What can be flagged is a space next to the apostrophe: the heading of §10.3 says "sin espacio di palabra", as does §10 (RULE_ORT_029). The severity is error for that reason, the same as RULE_ORT_034 (§10.3 point 1); the old rule pack had this point as "acceptable".
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 34.

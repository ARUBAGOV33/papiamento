## Rule

Words from Dutch, English and French, with their derivations and compounds, are written by the rules of the Papiamento orthography when a sound has come closer to Papiamento, a sound has dropped, or a sound has changed. The words listed as exceptions on pages 43-44 are written as printed.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §12.2 "Hulandes, Ingles y Frances", point 2, gazette page 42; exceptions on gazette pages 43-44. "Palabra cu ta bin di Hulandes, Ingles y Frances y e derivacion- y composicionnan ta skirbi segun e reglanan di “Ortografia di Papiamento” den caso cu:"

## Correct

- “boek” a bira “buki”
- “bicycle” a bira “bais”
- “brassière” a bira “brazier”

## Exceptions

- a. With their derivations and compounds (page 43): ambulance, alinea, bloemkool, champagne, chauffeur, crescent wrench, douche, gal, galop, gevoel, gewoon, geel, griep, haarspeld, handschoen, inkt, kerstboom, kool, koolrabi, krimp, lipstick, mannequin, mayonaise, miljard, paneermeel, penicilline, pensioen, pindakaas, pipe wrench, ring, sneeuw, steel, stoof, stoot, stroom, spook, spring, vakantie, veer, vies, verf, verkoud, verzwik, vloer, volley, vrij, washer, wax, website, windcharge, windshield, yeast, zuid, zuurkool
- Two options (page 43): blade/blet, leak / lik, tube/ chup, zwak /suak
- b. Dutch words that start with sch in Dutch (page 43). With sch: schaak, schaats, schors, schrift, schutter, schuur, schommel, schouder, schroef, schuif, schuin (see Notes), schizofrenia. With sc or sk: scandal, scarnir, scars, scop, scot, scral, scrap, skel, skit. Either: schaaf / scaf, schema / skema, scheur / sker, school / scol, schuw /skiu. Derivations keep the basic form, except those of "schema".
- c. Written as printed, derivations keep the basic form (page 43): aircondition, badbroek, benemuur, beschouder, boet, boormashin, chocomel, crocodil, choller, collect, dagvaar (see Notes), domverstand, fransebloem, gesprei, grif, ijscream, klapchi, kleinfeshi, lei (guia), longplay, nietmashin, piks, pinki, posterij, radiothon, ribchi, rooibiet, rumshop, signlight, snijbonchi, spiel, sprei, strooichi, touwboot, T-weg
- d. Words from brand or product names (page 44): clorox, coecoei, jello, jockey, keds, kleenex, kodak, kotex, Osterizer (see Notes), piccalilly, Q-tips

## Notes

- Decided errata, which take precedence over the printed lists: dagvaar is written dagvaard (err-0014); Osterizer is written osterizer (err-0015); schuin and scuins are both correct (err-0016).
- The exceptions are printed after point 3 under one heading "Excepcion:" without saying which point they belong to. They are listed here because they are the loans that keep a set spelling instead of following point 2.
- Footnote 1 (pages 42-43): the lists are not necessarily complete.
- The page splits some words across lines (ba-dbroek, kleinfes-hi); they are given above as whole words, badbroek and kleinfeshi, both headwords of the AB 2018 word list. The two-option pairs are copied with the page's own spacing.
- Point 3 (page 42), for words from Dutch start from the first person singular or the root: see RULE_ORT_054.
- The old rule pack had this as a warning; the law states it as a rule, so the severity here is error. A checker should flag only words it can match to the AB 2018 word list or `lexicon/loanwords/`.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on pages 42-44.

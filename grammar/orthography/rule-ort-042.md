## Rule

A compound made of a verb and a noun is written as one word when the two together have a single main stress.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §11.1 "Skirbimento hunto of separa relaciona cu verbo", point f, gazette page 35. "Ta skirbi composicion cu ta consisti di verbo y sustantivo hunto ora cu hunto nan tin un solo entonacion principal."

## Correct

- bisanieto, kibrahacha, pegasaya, wardacosta
- bringamosa, pasatempo, salbabida, wardalodo

## Notes

- The page refers on to §11.9 point 1a (gazette page 38, mal + verb; RULE_ORT_044) and §11.10 (gazette page 39, reduplication; RULE_ORT_047, RULE_ORT_048).
- "entonacion principal" is the main stress. A checker cannot hear stress, so it can only apply this rule to known compounds such as those listed.
- ORT2009 prints the same section on page 35.

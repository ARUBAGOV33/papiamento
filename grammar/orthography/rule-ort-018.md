## Rule

An appellative that appears together with a name is written with a capital letter.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.3 Skirbimento di nomber, point b, gazette page 30. "apelativo cu ta aparece cu un nomber,"

## Correct

- Mo Hose
- Chi Tonia
- Mama Maria
- Mama Teresa
- Papa Sha
- Don Pablo
- Señor Kock
- Sr. Figaroa
- Sra. Marcia

## Notes

- The page covers appellatives that appear with a name; it says nothing about the same words used without a name.
- Words of dignity and titles are §9.4 (gazette page 32), a separate rule.

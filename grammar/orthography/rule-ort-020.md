## Rule

Names of institutions, businesses, brands, organizations and the like are written with capital letters, except for conjunctions and prepositions. Such a name may also be written the way the organization itself has set it.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.3 Skirbimento di nomber, point f, gazette page 31. "nomber di institucion, negoshi, marca*, organisacion etc., cu excepcion di conhuncion y preposicion," "Nota: Por skirbi e nomber tambe segun e organisacion mes a stipula."

## Correct

- Cas di Cultura
- Scol di Arte
- Paradera Snack

## Exceptions

- Conjunctions and prepositions inside the name stay lowercase (di in the examples).
- The organization's own spelling of its name is also allowed (the Nota).

## Notes

- Severity is warning, not error: because an organization may set its own spelling, a checker cannot be sure a different capitalization is wrong.
- The asterisk on marca points to §12.2 point 2d (gazette page 44): words derived from brand or product names are written as listed there (e.g. clorox, kleenex, kodak). See err-0015 (Osterizer → osterizer) and RULE_ORT_052, RULE_ORT_053 and RULE_ORT_054.
- §8.1 b (gazette page 24): abbreviations that are proper names are written as the body or organization set them (C.I.A., havo, UNESCO).

## Rule

When a new topic begins after an ellipsis (three dots) that marks an interruption, the next word is written with a capital letter.

## Source

AB 2018 no. 68, annex Ortografia di Papiamento, §9.2.2 Tres punto, gazette page 29. "Ta skirbi cu mayusculo: ora ta cuminsa cu un tema nobo despues di tres punto cu ta indica un interupcion."

## Correct

- Sucu ta gaba ... E no a cambia ni un tiki.

## Notes

- Severity is warning: whether a new topic begins is a matter of meaning, so a checker should flag the case for review and not correct it.
- The page writes the three dots with a space on both sides.
- Related: RULE_ORT_013 (lowercase after an ellipsis inside a sentence).

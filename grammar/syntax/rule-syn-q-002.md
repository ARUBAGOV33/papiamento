## Rule
A direct question uses an interrogative pronoun, an interrogative adverb or any other word category at the start of the sentence. There are two kinds: questions expecting si or no as the answer, and questions expecting a sentence part as the answer. A si/no question has exactly the same elements as the corresponding statement; in writing it is distinguished by the question mark at the end, in speech by intonation. A question expecting a sentence part is introduced by an interrogative pronoun or adverb.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §6.2.1 "Frase interogativo directo", pp. 99–100; §6.2.1.1 "Frase interogativo cu si of no como contesta", p. 100; §6.2.1.2 "Frase interogativo cu un parti di frase como contesta", p. 101.
"Ta uza un pronomber interogativo, un adverbio interogativo of cualkier otro categoria di palabra na comienso di e frase pa haci pregunta"

## Correct
- Ken a mata e boa cu tabata drumi bou di e baranca? (pronomber interogativo)
- Dicon Pedrito no a pasa cerca mi siman pasa? (adverbio interogativo)
- (Ta) Manuel ta trahando un cas na Noord? (otro categoria: sustantivo)
- Bo ta bay e trip den bus awenochi?
- Marcia a laga traha un cash’i cushina nobo?
- E ruman di Bubu a haya trabou?
- E mucha a pasa su examen exitosamente? (frase interogativo cu si of no como contesta)
- E mucha a pasa su examen exitosamente. (frase enunciativo afirmativo)
- Ken a yama Buchi na telefon ayera? (pronomber interogativo como suheto)
- Kico e juffrouw a bisa bo siman pasa? (pronomber interogativo como obheto directo)
- Con bo por a subi e dak di e cas ey? (adverbio interogativo como complemento adverbial)

## Notes
- Severity is info: a description of how questions are formed, nothing to flag.
- The pair E mucha a pasa su examen exitosamente? / E mucha a pasa su examen exitosamente. shows that the question mark is the only written difference; see RULE_SYN_Q_003.
- In place of an interrogative adverb there can also be an interrogative adverbial expression (Nota, p. 99).
- p. 101 (Nota) also describes rhetorical questions, which expect no answer but express disapproval, irritation or an order, e.g. Bo kier a keda keto, por fabor?
- Questions with ta enfatico: RULE_SYN_Q_007. Classification: RULE_SYN_Q_001.

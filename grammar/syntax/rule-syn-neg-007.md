## Rule
Almost all the negation elements can be used together in one sentence.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §5.5.3 "Uzo di mas elemento di negacion den un frase", p. 97.
"Por uza casi tur e elementonan di negacion hunto den un frase"

## Correct
- Mi no kier nada di dje nunca mas tampoco.
- E no ta haci nada nunca tampoco.
- Hamas y nunca Maria ni Hose (no) a duna mi nada.
- Nunca e (no) sa regala ningun hende nada tampoco.

## Notes
- Severity is info: the book presents these sentences as correct, so a checker must not remove negation elements from them.
- The old pack added that such sentences "may still be stylistically heavy". The page says nothing about style, so that advice is not part of this rule.
- In the first two examples all the other elements follow the predicate and no is present (RULE_SYN_NEG_005). In the last two, Hamas y nunca or Nunca comes before the predicate and no is in brackets (optional, RULE_SYN_NEG_006), even though nada, ningun hende or tampoco also follow the predicate.
- Related: RULE_SYN_NEG_004 (double negation).

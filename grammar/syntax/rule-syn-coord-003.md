## Rule
In sentence coordination a coordinating conjunction joins independent sentences that have a logical relation to each other; each keeps its own complete meaning and does not depend on the other. The result is a compound coordinative sentence (frase componi coordinativo). By the kind of conjunction, it is copulative (a listing or addition), disjunctive (an alternative or dilemma) or adversative (an opposition or contrast).

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §3.1.2.3 "Coordinacion di frase", pp. 69–70, with I. Copulativo (p. 69), II. Disyuntivo and III. Adversativo (p. 70).
"Coordinacion di frase ta e construccion den cual un conhuncion coordinativo ta uni y relaciona frase independiente cu otro"

## Correct
- El a baña y el a bisti.
- Bo ta bay baña of bo ta bay drumi?
- Mi ta malo, pero mi ta bay traha.
- Copulative: Mi ta traha na computer y mi yiu ta wak television.
- Copulative: Ni Akira no ta bay cine ni Yeremias no ta bay stadion awe.
- Disjunctive: Sea bo ta studia e capitulo di preposicion, sea bo ta studia e capitulo di conhuncion.
- Disjunctive: Straño ta cu un ora mi ta sinti bon, otro ora mi ta sinti malo.
- Adversative: E ta perdi, pero e speransa t’ey cu por hay’e.
- Adversative: Tur hende tabata contra dje, sinembargo e no a flakia.

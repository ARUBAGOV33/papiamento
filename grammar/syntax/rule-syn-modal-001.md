## Rule
The complement of modality (complemento di modalidad) is an adverbial complement that generally refers to the whole sentence; it gives the speaker's opinion or judgement about what the sentence expresses.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §2.7.2.11 "Complemento di modalidad", p. 61.
"Generalmente complemento di modalidad ta referi na henter e frase; e ta trece un opinion of huicio di e hablante tocante loke e ta expresa"

## Correct
- Casualmente mi tin placa na banco ainda.
- Posiblemente Ministro Leo lo tin di biaha pa Hulanda.
- Probablemente e conferencia lo no sigui pa falta di interes.

## Notes
- Severity is info: a category for analysis, nothing to flag.
- The page refers to Chapter 8 for the full description. §8.3.2 (p. 120) adds that the modality complement can express both modality of feeling (sintimento) and modality of reasoning (rasonamento); §8.2 (p. 113) defines the two. The forms it can take: RULE_SYN_MODAL_002.
- The Nota on p. 61 says the adverbial complement is the most movable sentence part: generally at the start or end of the sentence, but also before or after the predicate or as an integral part of it.

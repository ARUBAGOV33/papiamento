## Rule
Ellipsis in a subordinate clause is leaving out its conjunction (pa or cu in the book's examples). It occurs in desiderative sentences and in other cases, and it generally shows up in spoken language.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §4.3 "Elipsis den frase subordina", pp. 86–87.
"Elipsis den frase subordina ta e fenomeno den cual ta omiti e conhuncion"
"Elipsis den frase subordina ta un fenomeno cu generalmente ta manifesta su mes den idioma papia."

## Correct
- Mi ta desea pa e bin.
- Mi ta desea e bin.
- Mi kier pa bo stop di papia.
- Mi kier bo stop di papia.
- Mi ta spera cu e t’ey.
- Mi ta spera e t’ey.
- Mi ta kere cu bo ta lesa bon.
- Mi ta kere bo ta lesa bon.
- Mi ta pensa cu ta facil pa siña Matematica.
- Mi ta pensa ta facil pa siña Matematica.

## Notes
- Severity is info. The book presents both forms of each pair without calling either one wrong, so a checker should not flag the missing conjunction and should not insert it.
- The page says only that the omission generally occurs in spoken language. It does not tell writers to restore the conjunction in formal writing; that is why the old pack's RULE_SYN_SUB_002 has no file.
- The first three pairs are desiderative sentences; the last two are the "other cases" the page mentions.

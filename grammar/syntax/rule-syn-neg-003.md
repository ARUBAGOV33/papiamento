## Rule
In negation of a sentence part, the speaker denies only what the marked part denotes, not the whole sentence. The negated part always comes at the start of the sentence, preceded by the negation element no together with ta enfatico (No ta …).

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §5.2.2 "Negacion di un parti di frase", p. 90, and §5.4.1 "Luga di e adverbio di negacion no", b, p. 92.
"E parti di frase cu ta marca cu negacion ta bin semper na cuminsamento di e frase, precedi pa e elemento di negacion hunto cu ta enfatico"

## Correct
- No ta Daniel ta bende su auto.
- No ta ayera Jason a come.
- No ta na hotel Maria tabata traha antes.
- No ta mi tanta ta bende e cas.
- No ta e television cu pantaya plat mi a cumpra.
- No ta ayera mi mama a traha e bolo di fruta.

## Notes
- Severity is info. The old pack had warning ("do not paraphrase as whole-sentence negation"); that is advice for rewriting tools, not something the page tells a writer to fix. A rewriter should still keep the two meanings apart: No ta Daniel ta bende su auto. means someone other than Daniel is selling his car (p. 90), while Daniel no ta bende su auto. negates the whole sentence (RULE_SYN_NEG_002).
- The old pack's title mentioned a form "nota". No page prints it: the text layer of p. 17 reads "Nota su nieta", but the printed page has "No ta su nieta" (§1.5.1.2). Only No ta, two words, is supported.
- The same construction is described under ta enfatico in negative statements, §1.5.1.2, pp. 16–17 (no goes before ta enfatico).
- A direct question that starts with No ta is not necessarily a negative sentence; the book treats it as modality (§1.5.2.1, Nota 2, p. 18).
- Stilistica (STI2008, PDF page 3, "Secuencia di frase") gives style advice on word order around no and tur; see RULE_STI_ORDER_001. (The old pack's RULE_STY_SEQ_002 has no file; RULE_STI_ORDER_001 covers that page.)

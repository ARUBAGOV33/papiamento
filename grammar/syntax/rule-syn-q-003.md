## Rule
In writing, a question is marked with a question mark at the end of the sentence. An indirect question does not take a question mark at the end of the sentence.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §6.1 "Introduccion", p. 99; §6.2.1.1, p. 100; §6.2.2 "Frase interogativo indirecto", pp. 101–102.
"Den lenga skirbi ta indica interogacion cu signo di interogacion na final di frase"
"No ta uza signo di pregunta na final di e frase"

## Correct
- Bo por pasa mi e tayo?
- Ken a cumpra e cas situa na Savaneta?
- Dicon e compania a kita Julia for di trabou?
- Juffrouw no a bisa nos kico nos mester haci pa examen. (indirect question, no question mark)
- Mama a puntra mi ta cu cua bus mi a bin cas. (indirect question, no question mark)
- Papa no a laga mi sa si e ta bay pisca mañan. (indirect question, no question mark)

## Notes
- Severity is error: a direct question without a question mark, or an indirect question ending in one, breaks what the page states. The book prints no wrong sentence, so there is no Incorrect example.
- A si/no question has the same words as the statement (E mucha a pasa su examen exitosamente? / E mucha a pasa su examen exitosamente., p. 100), so a checker cannot tell from the words alone that a sentence is meant as a question. It can reliably check only sentences that start with an interrogative pronoun or adverb, and even then must exclude indirect questions, which sit inside a larger sentence.
- The question mark is also stated in §1.4.3.2, p. 12 ("Den idioma skirbi ta pone e signo di interogacion na final di frase").
- The book also prints ?! at the end of a question, e.g. Ta ki manera esaki ta?! (p. 18), so a checker should accept ?! as well.

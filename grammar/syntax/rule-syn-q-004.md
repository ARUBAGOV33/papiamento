## Rule
There are two kinds of indirect question: one introduced by an interrogative pronoun or interrogative adverb, and one introduced by the conjunction si. With si, the interrogative effect falls on the whole clause that follows; with an interrogative pronoun or adverb, it falls on one sentence part in the subordinate clause.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §6.2.2 "Frase interogativo indirecto", p. 101; §6.2.2.1, p. 102; §6.2.2.2 "Frase interogativo indirecto introduci pa e conhuncion si", p. 103.
"Den caso di frase interogativo indirecto introduci pa e conhuncion si e efecto interogativo ta cay riba henter e frase cu ta siguie"

## Correct
- Juan no sa si e ta bay come cu su amigonan.
- El a puntra si gobierno ta bay tuma medida.
- E no a bisa si ta un chuchubi of ta un trupial el a tende canta.
- Papa no a laga mi sa si e ta bay pisca mañan.
- Alex sa exactamente ken a horta e cadena di oro for di su camber. (pronomber interogativo)
- Nos no kier a puntra e homber kico e ta bay haci. (pronomber interogativo)
- Mi ta pensa con ta soluciona e problema aki. (adverbio interogativo)

## Notes
- Severity is info: a description of how indirect questions are formed, nothing to flag.
- The old pack limited this rule to yes/no questions with si; the book describes both kinds, so both are listed here.
- In certain cases si is replaced by cu: RULE_SYN_Q_005.
- Indirect questions take no question mark: RULE_SYN_Q_003.
- In the pronoun/adverb kind, the interrogative pronoun is the subject or object of the subordinate clause and the interrogative adverb is an adverbial complement (p. 102).

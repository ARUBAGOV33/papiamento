## Rule
In certain cases the conjunction si that introduces an indirect question is replaced by the conjunction cu.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §6.2.2.2 "Frase interogativo indirecto introduci pa e conhuncion si", p. 103.
"Den cierto caso sa reemplasa e conhuncion si pa e conhuncion cu"

## Correct
- E no a bisa si ta un chuchubi of ta un trupial el a tende canta.
- E no a bisa cu ta un chuchubi of ta un trupial el a tende canta.

## Notes
- Severity is info. The book gives the cu version as a normal alternative, so a checker must not change cu to si here. The old pack's warning has no basis on the page.
- The page does not say which cases allow the replacement; it gives only this one pair. A checker should therefore neither require nor suggest cu.

## Rule
Interrogative sentences are classified as direct or indirect. A direct question is an independent sentence. An indirect question always appears as a subordinate clause in a complex sentence, where it is the direct object of that sentence.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §6.2 (classification) and §6.2.1 "Frase interogativo directo", p. 99; §6.2.2 "Frase interogativo indirecto", p. 101.
"Ta clasifica frase interogativo den dos clase: frase interogativo directo y frase interogativo indirecto"
"Frase interogativo indirecto semper ta aparece como frase subordina den un frase subordinativo."

## Correct
- Ken a yama mi na mi celular? (directo)
- Mi no sa mas kico pa haci pa por complac’e. (indirecto)

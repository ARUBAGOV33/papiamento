## Rule
When the other negation element appears before the predicate, the negation element no is optional: the sentence is correct with or without it.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §5.3 "Elemento di negacion", p. 91, and §5.5.2 "Otro elemento di negacion dilanti di e predicado", p. 97.
"Ora cu e otro elemento(nan) di negacion ta aparece dilanti di e predicado, e uzo di e elemento di negacion no ta opcional"

## Correct
- Hamas y nunca e famia ey lo (no) bende e tereno patras di nan cas.
- Ningun hende (no) sa ki dia e conferencia lo tuma luga.
- Ni alcohol e (no) ta bebe mas.
- Hamas e señora honesto ey lo (no) traiciona su casa.
- Ni Corsou e muchanan di atletismo (no) por a bay.
- Nada (no) ta interesa e mucha mas.

## Notes
- The old rule pack had this rule reversed: it said no is required when the other negation element precedes the predicate, with severity error. Both p. 91 and p. 97 say no is optional here, so this file follows the book, and a checker must flag neither the presence nor the absence of no in this position.
- The book writes the optional no in brackets: each example stands for two correct sentences, one with no and one without.
- Hamas y nunca is also the Stilistica example of tautology used for emphasis (STI2008, PDF page 2); see RULE_STY_TAUT_002.

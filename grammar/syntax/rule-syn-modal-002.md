## Rule
Different constructions can act as a complement (adverbial) of modality: an adverb or adverbial expression; a preposition (prepositional phrase); a subordinate clause, introduced by the conjunction manera; or an interjection. An interjection almost always expresses modality of feeling.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §8.3.2 "Expresion di modalidad pa medio di e complemento adverbial", pp. 120–121.
"Ta distingui diferente tipo di construccion cu por fungi como complemento di modalidad"

## Correct
- Adverb: Ta apenas tres or.
- Adverb: Hasta e polis mester a hari.
- Adverbial expression: Mi tin hopi ora ta warda caba.
- Preposition: Na mi opinion tur hende tin e mesun derecho.
- Preposition: Segun nan ta bisa e situacion a mehora.
- Preposition: Na mi parecer el a haya suficiente ora liber.
- Subordinate clause with manera: Manera ta parce, e ta bayendo bon na scol.
- Interjection: Dios gracia, ta fin di siman.
- Interjection: Holo, e luga ta yen yen.
- Interjection: Caramba, bo por a yama mi prome.

## Rule
A direct object complement specifies or qualifies the direct object. Because it depends on a direct object, it always occurs in a sentence whose predicate has a transitive independent verb as its nucleus.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §2.8.1 "Definicion", p. 62, and §2.8.5 "Complemento di obheto directo", pp. 64–65.
"E complemento di obheto directo ta specifica of cualifica e obheto directo."
"ta trata semper di frase den cua ta uza un verbo independiente transitivo como nucleo di e predicado"

## Correct
- Hopi hende ta considera Betico Croes un heroe.
- E obreronan a verf tur e portanan colo di oro.
- Hues a declara e acusadonan culpabel.
- Awe e director a haya e bailarinanan mas contento.
- E mucha muhe a mira su yoncuman sinta den park.

## Notes
- Severity is info: an analysis rule; the book prints no wrong sentence.
- In the examples the direct objects are Betico Croes, tur e portanan, e acusadonan, e bailarinanan and su yoncuman; the object complements are un heroe, colo di oro, culpabel, mas contento and sinta den park.
- §2.8.1 (pp. 61–62) says an object complement can go with a direct, indirect or prepositional object, but the book then limits itself to the direct object complement. The old pack's wording ("object complements") is wider than what the page explains; this rule covers only the direct object complement.
- p. 63 explains why colo di oro is an object complement and not an adverbial complement: it describes the doors, not the painting.

## Rule
Every interrogative sentence can be emphasised with ta enfatico. Ta enfatico stands directly before the sentence part it emphasises.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §6.3, Nota, p. 103.
"Por enfatisa tur frase interogativo pa medio di ta enfatico. E ta enfatico ta aparece dilanti di e parti di frase cu ta enfatisa"

## Correct
- (Ta) Marcia a laga traha un cash’i cushina nobo?
- (Ta) Ken a yama Buchi na telefon ayera?
- Papa no a laga mi sa si (ta) e ta bay pisca mañan.

## Notes
- Severity is info: the questions are correct with or without ta, so a checker must not strip ta enfatico or flag it as a doubled particle.
- More examples are in §1.5.2 "Den frase interogativo directo", pp. 17–18, e.g. Ta Madushi a duna su nieta un popchi pa su cumpleaño? and, with an interrogative word, Ta ken ta na porta?, Ta na unda bo ta bay?, Ta cuant’or e ta pensa di yega?
- When the predicate is emphasised, the verb is repeated but the particle is not: Ta duna Madushi a duna su nieta un popchi pa su cumpleaño? (pp. 16–17, Nota 1). With an auxiliary plus independent verb, either verb can be repeated: Ta mester mi mester haci e tarea ey? (p. 18, Nota 3).
- Ta enfatico has no grammatical function and is not a sentence part; it forms part of the sentence part it emphasises (§1.5, p. 16).
- The third example is an indirect question, which takes no question mark (RULE_SYN_Q_003).
- Related: RULE_SYN_NEG_003 (No ta …), RULE_GRA_MODAL_TA_001 (ta enfatico before a modal is not the present particle).

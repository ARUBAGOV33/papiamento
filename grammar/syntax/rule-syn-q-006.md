## Rule
Ellipsis in an interrogative sentence is leaving out words, word groups or sentence parts.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §6.3 "Elipsis den frase interogativo", p. 103.
"Elipsis den frase interogativo ta e fenomeno den cual ta omiti palabra, grupo di palabra of parti di frase"

## Correct
- (Bo ta) Contento awo?
- Kico bo kier come of (kico bo kier) bebe?
- Bo kier pastechi di carni of (bo kier pastechi) di keshi?

## Notes
- Severity is info: the questions are correct with or without the bracketed words, so a checker must not flag the shorter form or insert the missing words.
- The page points to §1.4.3.6 "Frase eliptico" (p. 14), which says the left-out words can be deduced and understood from the context. The old pack's "where recoverable" matches that; its limits to a "conversational register" and "teaching mode" are not on the page.
- The text layer of p. 103 reads "(Bota) Contento awo?"; the printed page has "(Bo ta)".
- Related: RULE_SYN_SUB_001 (ellipsis of the conjunction in subordinate clauses).

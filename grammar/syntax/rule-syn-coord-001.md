## Rule
Coordination joins two or more words, word groups or independent sentences with a coordinating conjunction. When words or word groups are coordinated, they always have the same syntactic function and the same semantic value. When sentences are coordinated, they have a logical relation to each other.

## Source
Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Sintaxis (2015), §3.1.1 "Introduccion", p. 67; §3.1.2.1 "Coordinacion di palabra", p. 68; §3.1.2.2 "Coordinacion di grupo di palabra", pp. 68–69.
"Den caso di palabra of grupo di palabra, semper e palabranan of e gruponan di palabra tin e mesun funcion sintactico y e mesun balor semantico."

## Correct
- Willem y Juan ta bay biblioteca otro siman. (coordinacion di suheto)
- E ta yora of (ta) rabia cada rato. (coordinacion di predicado)
- Nan kier cumpra cas y auto. (coordinacion di obheto directo)
- E cas cora y e cas geel ta di mi tata. (coordinacion di suheto)
- E regla aki ta valido tanto pa scol publico como pa scol priva. (coordinacion di obheto preposicional)
- Ni e sapato oraño ni e tas cora (no) a keda bon cu e shimis berde cu e tabatin bisti. (coordinacion di suheto)

## Notes
- Severity is info. The old rule pack made this an error ("flag mismatched coordination"), but Sintaxis states it as the defining property of coordination and prints no wrong example.
- p. 68 explains the examples: Willem and Juan are both subject and both human beings; yora and rabia are both the nucleus of the predicate and both emotional expressions; cas and auto are both direct object and both concrete objects.
- Nota on p. 68: in E ta yora of (ta) rabia cada rato. the particle of the second predicate is optional.
- The style advice that items in an enumeration should be of the same kind comes from Stilistica (STI2008, PDF page 4, "Simetria/Asimetria") and is the rule a checker could flag as style: RULE_STY_ASYM_002.

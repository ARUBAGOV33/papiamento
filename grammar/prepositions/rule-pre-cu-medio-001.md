## Rule
cu can mark a means or instrument used to do something.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.3 Cu, V. Medio, printed page 5.
"Cu por indica un medio of instrumento pa haci algo:"

## Correct
- Tirso a basha e cadushi abou cu un machete.
- Nan a transporta e heridonan cu ambulance pa hospital.
- Cu un tractor nan a rosa henter e mondi.
- Por fabor, yena e formulario aki cu potlood y no cu pen.

## Notes
- Other uses of cu: RULE_PRE_CU_COMP_001. na also has a relation of means (1.3.7 Na, IV, p. 16, e.g. "Lito ta bay scol na pia.").
- Describes a use only; nothing to flag.

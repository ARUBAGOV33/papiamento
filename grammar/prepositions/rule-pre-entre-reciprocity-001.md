## Rule

entre can mark a relation that two entities have with each other (reciprocity).

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.6 Entre, III. Relacion di reciprocidad, printed p. 13.
"Den e caso aki entre ta indica un relacion entre dos entidad pa cu otro"

## Correct

- Entre Wan cu Greta tin un relacion straño.
- Laga e cos keda entre nos.
- Entre nan dos cos no ta andando bon.
- Den fisica conoce e fenomeno di atraccion magnetico entre obheto di metal.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- Example 1 joins the two entities with cu; Nota 3 (p. 14) says two coordinated elements after entre take y (or cu). See RULE_PRE_ENTRE_001.

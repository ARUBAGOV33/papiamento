## Rule
In its relation of reference, na refers to a person's interpretation of an event or situation, as in na mi opinion and na mi parecer.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.7 Na, VI. Relacion di referencia, printed page 17.
"Den e relacion aki na ta referi na e interpretacion di un persona di un suceso of situacion"

## Correct
- Na mi opinion nos mester reuni mas biaha pa siman.
- Nos ta laga e decision na su bon criterio.
- Na mi idea b’a baha un tiki.
- Na mi parecer e mucha muhe ey ta na estado.
- Mi a tuma bo proposicionnan na consideracion.

## Notes
- The page does not say any other preposition is wrong in these expressions, so the old rule pack's "replace with na" correction is not supported and this rule flags nothing.
- Sintaxis (SIN2015) 8.3.2 Expresion di modalidad pa medio di e complemento adverbial, pp. 120–121, gives "Na mi opinion tur hende tin e mesun derecho." and "Na mi parecer el a haya suficiente ora liber." as complements of modality built with a preposition. The old pack's pp. 134–135 for this are PDF page numbers.

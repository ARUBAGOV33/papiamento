## Rule

pa can mark the agent of the action in a passive construction, or in an active construction with a resultative aspect.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.8 Pa, IV. Relacion di agente, printed p. 19, with the examples on p. 20.
"Den e relacion di agente pa ta indica e agente di e accion den un construccion pasivo of un construccion activo cu un aspecto resultativo"

## Correct

- E isla aki ta habita pa homber salvahe.
- E novela ta skirbi pa Denis Henriquez.
- E bentana a wordo kibra cu un piedra pa un choller.
- Su plannan tabata conoci pa tur hende.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- Keep apart from pa for cause (RULE_PRE_PA_CAUSE_001) and for the one something is meant for (RULE_PRE_PA_BENEFICIARY_001).
- The rule pack cited pp. 29-31; those are PDF pages. Printed page = PDF page - 10 (see sources/cards/PRE2016.md).

## Rule

na can mark the means used to carry out an action.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.7 Na, IV. Relacion di medio, printed p. 16.
"Den e relacion aki na ta indica e medio uza pa realisa e accion"

## Correct

- Lito ta bay scol na pia.
- Federico a gatia na man cu pia yega cerca.
- Mama a cose e disfraz na man.
- Jose a cuminsa skirbi storia na Papiamento.
- Bo ta ripara cu e piesa ey ta traha na mashin.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- Other senses of na: RULE_PRE_NA_SPACE_001, RULE_PRE_NA_TIME_001, RULE_PRE_NA_MODE_001, RULE_PRE_NA_QUANTITY_001, RULE_PRE_NA_OPINION_001. cu also marks means: RULE_PRE_CU_MEDIO_001.

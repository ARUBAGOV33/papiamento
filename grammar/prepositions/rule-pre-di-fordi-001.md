## Rule
In the book's literal examples of di marking origin in space, the compound preposition for di can be used instead of di. The book says the same of its examples of di marking the starting point of a span of time.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.5 Di, II. Relacion di espacio (Nota 1) and III. Relacion di tempo (Nota 2), printed page 9.
"Den tur e ehempelnan anterior por uza e preposicion componi for di na luga di e preposicion simpel di."
"Den e ehempelnan aki tambe por cambia di pa e preposicion componi for di."

## Correct
- Nos bisiñanan a caba di yega di Colombia.
- El a cana di Playa te San Nicolas.
- Vakantie grandi ta dura di juli te augustus.

## Notes
- The page prints these examples with di only; it does not print the versions with for di.
- Nota 1 comes before the figurative spatial examples (e.g. "Nelo ta Arubiano di nacemento, pero el a lanta na Hulanda."), so the page does not say for di works there. No such note is given for the other senses of di (possession, material, quantity, content, reference).
- Both di and for di are correct here, so there is nothing to flag.

## Rule

In its spatial sense, na generally marks a place in space, whether that place has no dimension or one or more dimensions. The spatial sense also has an abstract or figurative use.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.7 Na, I. Relacion di espacio, printed p. 15.
"E preposicion na den su relacion di espacio, por lo general, ta indica un luga den espacio, sea sin dimension of cu un of mas dimension"

## Correct

Spatial:

- Colga e cadena na e haak.
- Francisco ta biba na Venezuela.
- No wak na laira, bo ta spanta e tempo.
- E ta na caminda pa cas.
- Fabio a tira su mes na vloer y cuminsa grita.
- Na Isla Trading tin baratio cu hopi mercancia di bon calidad.

Abstract or figurative:

- Mi kier sa cu Fabio ta tene mi na liña cu e asunto di pago di e cas.
- Si bo liña ta na awa, bo tin chens di gana.
- Ya ta dos siman caba cu Andres a cay na cama.
- Annie ta na hal’i palo pa sa si su examen a bay bon.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- §1.3.7 lists seven relations for na: espacio, tempo, modo, medio, cantidad, destinacion and referencia. See RULE_PRE_NA_TIME_001, RULE_PRE_NA_MODE_001, RULE_PRE_NA_MEDIO_001, RULE_PRE_NA_QUANTITY_001 and RULE_PRE_NA_OPINION_001.
- Compare den (PRE2016 §1.3.4, p. 6), which marks an entity in a place with two or three dimensions that is closed or limited. This matters for RULE_PRE_EN_001.

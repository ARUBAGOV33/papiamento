## Rule
cu marks a relation of company. The book calls this the basic and most frequent relation that cu expresses.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.3 Cu, I. Relacion di compania, printed page 4.
"E relacion basico y mas frecuente cu e preposicion cu ta indica ta e relacion di compania"

## Correct
- El a bay cine cu su mama.
- Mi ta contento cu boso ta biaha cu nos.
- Pedro ta gusta come pan cu keshi cu koffie cu lechi.
- E ladron a baha na awa cu e caha di placa.

## Notes
- 1.3.3 lists the uses of cu: company, characteristic, manner, circumstance, means, and fixed preposition of certain verbs. See RULE_PRE_CU_CHARACTERISTIC_001, RULE_PRE_CU_MODE_001, RULE_PRE_CU_CIRCUMSTANCE_001, RULE_PRE_CU_MEDIO_001, RULE_PRE_CU_FIXED_001 and RULE_PRE_CU_NE_001.
- Describes a use only; nothing to flag.

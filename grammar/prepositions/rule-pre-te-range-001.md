## Rule
te marks a relation of space or of time. In space it marks the end point of a path; in time it marks the end point of a span. The spatial use can also be figurative.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.10 Te, printed pages 23–24.
"Den e relacion di espacio te ta indica un punto final di un trayecto espacial"
"Den e relacion aki te ta indica un punto final di un trayecto temporal"

## Correct
- Pa bo yega e liña ecuatorial bo mester bula bay te Ecuador.
- E caminata ta di Westpunt te Punta Basora.
- Bo tin cu studia e buki aki te capitulo 6.
- Su animo a baha te suela.
- El a traha riba su investigacion di januari te maart.
- Sonia lo keda Hulanda te fin di luna.

## Notes
- Several of the page's examples give the starting point with di (di … te …). For di as the starting point see RULE_PRE_DI_ORIGIN_001 and RULE_PRE_DI_TIME_001.
- The two time examples with the shortened clock form cinc’or (p. 24) are not copied, because `_meta/invariants.md` (AB §11.8, p. 38) writes clock times as numeral and or separately. See the same point in RULE_PRE_ENTRE_001.
- The old rule pack made this an error with "insert te". The page only describes the use, so nothing is flagged.

## Rule

Within its spatial relation, pa can also mark the destinatario: the person or thing that the first entity is meant for. The book gives abstract or figurative uses of this, marked (b).

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.8 Pa, I. Relacion di espacio, point b, printed p. 18.
"b. e destinatario di loke e prome entidad ta referi na dje"

## Correct

- E tata a cumpra un cacho chikito pa su yiu.
- Papa a pone un trampa pa e djaca.
- E buki aki ta pa hobennan.
- Nos a cumpra un lampi pa e sala.
- Mi mester cumpra un taira nobo pa mi auto.
- Corda tur mi esfuerso y mi sacrificionan pa bo educacion.
- Tur mi amor ta pa bo so.
- Tur su cariño tabata pa e yiunan so.

Abstract or figurative, marked (b):

- Fruta ta bon pa salud.
- Mi ta duna bo un conseho pa bo mes bon.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- The book treats this as point b of I. Relacion di espacio, not as a relation of its own. Point a (direction) is RULE_PRE_PA_DIRECTION_001.
- na also marks a destinatario (RULE_PRE_NA_QUANTITY_001, p. 17).
- The rule pack cited pp. 28-29; those are PDF pages. Printed page = PDF page - 10 (see sources/cards/PRE2016.md).

## Rule

In its time sense, pa marks a point or a certain period in time.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.8 Pa, II. Relacion di tempo, printed p. 19.
"Den e relacion aki pa ta indica un punto of cierto periodo den tempo"

## Correct

- P. G. a bay cera pa cuater aña.
- Bo n’ por kere cu mi ta caba e trabou di awe pa mañan!
- Pa cuarto pa shete bo n’ ta haya mi na cas mas.
- Pa basta tempo caba e ta malo.
- Beatris ta bay yega un ora pa otro.
- Pa añanan largo e prizonero cu a huy a keda fugitivo.
- E dia aki lo keda pa semper graba den mi memoria.
- Con e mucha ey por a cambia asina di un dia pa otro!

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- Example 1 is copied from the scan, which prints a space between the initials ("P. G."); the text layer runs them together as "P.G.".
- pa between two clock times after entre is a separate matter: RULE_PRE_ENTRE_001 calls entre … pa … incorrect. na also marks a point or period in time: RULE_PRE_NA_TIME_001.
- The rule pack cited pp. 28-29; those are PDF pages. Printed page = PDF page - 10 (see sources/cards/PRE2016.md).

## Rule
cu can mark manner.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.3 Cu, III Modo, printed page 5.
"Cu por indica modo:"

## Correct
- Tur e añanan aki e studiantenan a traha cu gran perseverancia.
- Mira cu ki atencion e ta haciendo su tareanan.
- Nos a duna nos comentario cu sintimento mixto.

## Notes
- Other uses of cu: RULE_PRE_CU_COMP_001. na also has a relation of manner (1.3.7 Na, III, p. 16).
- The page's example with "cu mucho gusto" is not copied: `_meta/invariants.md` rules hopi, not mucho, for "a lot" (see barbarismos bar-0004).
- Describes a use only; nothing to flag.

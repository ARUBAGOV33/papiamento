## Rule
di is used with certain nouns that are singular in form but plural in meaning (collective nouns). The book's examples are bodogo, cacho, man, mancha, tou, troshi and trupa.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.5.1 Di cu sustantivo colectivo, printed pages 11–12.
"Ta uza di cu cierto sustantivo cu un forma singular, pero cu un nificacion plural."

## Correct
- Nanzi tabata loer e bodogo di siboyo den hofi di Shon Arey.
- Un trupa di wolf a ataca un bina bieu den mondi.
- Awendia bo no ta haya un cacho di banana pa cumpra.
- Ya cu bo ta bay supermercado bo por cumpra un man di bacoba y un troshi di druif pa mi.

## Notes
- The book introduces the seven nouns as "Algun ehempel", so the list is not closed.
- The old rule pack made this a warning. The page only describes the construction and prints no incorrect form, so nothing is flagged.

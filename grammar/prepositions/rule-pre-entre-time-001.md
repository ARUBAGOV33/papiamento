## Rule

In its time sense, entre marks a moment or period that lies between two points in time.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.6 Entre, II. Relacion di tempo, printed p. 13.
"Den e relacion aki entre ta indica un momento of periodo intermedio"

## Correct

- E fiesta lo tuma luga entre tres or y nueb’or.
- Carnaval semper ta cay entre Aña Nobo y Diaranson di Shinishi.
- Marduga ta cay entre meyanochi y kibra di dia.
- E avion a sali entre cuarto pasa di ocho y och’or y mey.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- Nota 4 (p. 14) shows the common mistake entre … pa … with a time range and calls it incorrect; use y (or cu). That is its own rule, RULE_PRE_ENTRE_001, and is not repeated here.
- nueb’or and och’or do not break the clock-time invariant ("7 or"): AB 2018 §10.1 point 3 (p. 33) requires exactly these apostrophe forms (cuat'or, cinc'or, shet'or, och'or, nueb'or, cuant'or), and §11.8 point 3 (p. 38) refers back to it.
- Diaranson di Shinishi is the name of a religious day. AB 2018 §9.3 point i (p. 31) capitalises feast and religious days (Aña Nobo, Semana Santa), so it does not conflict with lowercase day names (§9.6, p. 32).
- Related time ranges: RULE_PRE_DI_TIME_001 and RULE_PRE_TE_RANGE_001 (di … te …).
- The rule pack cited pp. 22-24; those are PDF pages. Printed page = PDF page - 10 (see sources/cards/PRE2016.md).

## Rule

pa can mark the exchange of one entity for another.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.8 Pa, V. Relacion di intercambio, printed p. 20.
"Den e relacion aki pa ta indica e cambio di un entidad pa otro entidad"

## Correct

- M’a cumpra e buki pa 25 florin.
- E Indjannan tabata troca cocolishi pa mercancia.
- Mi mester bay banco pa cambia florin pa dollar.
- Mucha gusta cambia bals pa kiniki.
- Ken lo kier cambia su propio yiu pa yiu di otro hende.
- Mi no ta bende mi inteligencia pa nada di mundo.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- na can also go with an amount of money (Mi ta lag’e na 75 florin pa bo., RULE_PRE_NA_QUANTITY_001, p. 17).
- The rule pack cited pp. 29-31; those are PDF pages. Printed page = PDF page - 10 (see sources/cards/PRE2016.md).

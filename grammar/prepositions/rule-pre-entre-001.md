## Rule
entre is used before a coordination of two elements or before a plural noun. When it comes before two coordinated elements, they are joined with the conjunction y (or with cu). Joining them with pa is incorrect.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.6 Entre, printed pages 12–14. The rule is in Notas 2–4 on p. 14; the correct examples below are from 1.3.6 on pp. 12–14.
"Si tene cuenta cu Nota 3, por compronde cu esaki ta incorecto."
"Ta recomenda pa no haci uzo di e combinacion cu e preposicion pa, pero cu e conhuncion y (of e preposicion cu)."

## Correct
- Savaneta ta keda entre Pos Chikito y Cura Cabay.
- Marduga ta cay entre meyanochi y kibra di dia.
- Entre Wan cu Greta tin un relacion straño.
- Bo mester scoge entre e diferente opcionnan cu tin.
- E fiesta lo tuma luga entre tres or y nueb’or.

## Incorrect
- E fiesta lo tuma luga entre 10 or pa 3 or. → join the two times with y (or cu) instead of pa. The page prints no corrected version of this sentence.

## Notes
- The time example from p. 13 uses nueb’or, the apostrophe form AB 2018 §10.1.3 (gazette page 33) prescribes for clock times; see `lexicon/numbers/times.tsv`. The page prints a curly apostrophe; the data files use a straight one.
- pa has many correct uses. A checker should flag pa only when it joins the two members that follow entre.

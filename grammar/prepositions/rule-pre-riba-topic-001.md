## Rule
riba marks a relation of space, in which the first entity is or is not in contact with a horizontal or vertical surface; it can also mark direction, and the spatial use can be figurative. riba also marks manner, the way the action of the predicate takes place. riba can also be the fixed preposition of a verb.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.9 Riba, printed pages 21–22.
"E preposicion aki ta denota un relacion di espacio y un relacion di modo."
"Tambe riba por ta preposicion fiho di verbo:"

## Correct
- E caliz ta riba altar den misa.
- E homber pelon no tin cabey riba su cabes.
- E avion a pasa riba e cas.
- E porta di su cas ta habri riba caya.
- Mi tin e palabra riba mi punt’i lenga.
- E baby ta cana riba su tenchi.
- Ora bo haya pinda, corda riba mi.
- Tur anochi e mama ta pensa riba su yiu na Colombia.

## Notes
- The Nota on p. 23 sends the reader to Nota 3 under 1.3.5 (p. 11): in a relation of reference, di is correct and riba is not. See RULE_PRE_DI_REF_001. The uses above are where riba is right, so a checker applying that rule should leave them alone.
- The verbs the page shows with fixed riba are papia riba, corda riba, pensa riba and elabora riba. It gives them as examples, not a closed list.
- Describes uses only; nothing to flag.

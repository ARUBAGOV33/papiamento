## Rule

pa can introduce the norm or standard against which something is compared or judged.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.8 Pa, VI. Relacion di comparacion, printed p. 20.
"Den e caso aki e complemento preposicional ta implica e norma standard di comparacion"

## Correct

- Pa un Hulandes di e tempo aya e tabata papia bon Papiamento.
- Pa su edad e mucha aki ta demasiado spierto.
- Pa su calidad mediocre e co’i hunga ta caro.
- Polis a weita nos pa ladron.
- Pa sabi cu bo ta, toch bo ta kiboca.
- Pa un delantero Ronaldo ta core pocopoco awendia.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- Example 4 on the page is left out because it uses mucho for "a lot", which `_meta/invariants.md` replaces with hopi.
- For comparison with entre see RULE_PRE_ENTRE_COMPARISON_001.

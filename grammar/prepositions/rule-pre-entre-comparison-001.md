## Rule

entre can introduce a comparison between two or more entities.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.6 Entre, V. Relacion di comparacion, printed p. 14.
"Den e relacion aki entre ta introduci un comparacion entre dos of mas entidad"

## Correct

- Entre tur e hendenan ey mi no por a distingui mi amigo.
- Entre tur su yiunan Diego ta esun menos arogante.
- Tin un gran diferencia entre chimpanse y gorila.
- Bo mester scoge entre e diferente opcionnan cu tin.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- Nota 2 (p. 14): the meaning of entre means it is used, as the examples show, before a coordination of two elements or before a noun in the plural. Notas 3 and 4: two coordinated elements are joined with y (or cu), not pa; see RULE_PRE_ENTRE_001.
- Other senses of entre: RULE_PRE_ENTRE_SPACE_001, RULE_PRE_ENTRE_TIME_001, RULE_PRE_ENTRE_RECIPROCITY_001, RULE_PRE_ENTRE_STATE_001.

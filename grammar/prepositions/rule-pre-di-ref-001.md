## Rule
di can mark a relation of reference, in which the first entity concerns or refers to the second. The book warns that this referential di is often wrongly replaced by riba, and says that riba would not be correct in its examples.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.5 Di, VII. Relacion di referencia and Nota 3, printed page 11.
"Den e relacion di referencia e prome entidad ta concerni of referi na e di dos entidad"
"Den e ehempelnan anterior e uzo di riba lo no ta corecto!"

## Correct
- Dr. R. Garcia a haci un estudio di e malesanan tropical.
- Lo tene un referendum na october pa bin cu un reforma di nos Constitucion.
- E profesor Italiano aki tin un conocemento profundo di e teoria ey.
- Un articulo tendencioso den un corant por influencia un persona su interpretacion di un suceso of situacion.

## Notes
- The page prints no sentence with the wrong riba; it only says riba would be incorrect in the four examples above. The incorrect sentence in the old rule pack was not on the page and is not copied here.
- A checker needs the meaning to apply this: riba is correct for space, manner and after verbs such as pensa riba (RULE_PRE_RIBA_TOPIC_001). The Nota under 1.3.9 Riba (p. 23) points back to this Nota 3.
- na also has a relation of reference (1.3.7 Na, VI, p. 17: na mi opinion, na mi parecer); see RULE_PRE_NA_OPINION_001. Nota 3 is only about di versus riba.
- Other senses of di in 1.3.5: RULE_PRE_DI_POSSESSION_001, RULE_PRE_DI_ORIGIN_001, RULE_PRE_DI_TIME_001, RULE_PRE_DI_MATERIAL_001, RULE_PRE_DI_QUANTITY_001, RULE_PRE_DI_CONTENT_001.

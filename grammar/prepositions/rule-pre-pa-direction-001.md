## Rule

In its spatial sense, pa can mark movement or orientation in a certain direction. The book also gives abstract or figurative uses of this sense, marked (a).

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.8 Pa, I. Relacion di espacio, point a, printed p. 18; the abstract variants marked (a) are on p. 19.
"a. movecion of orientacion den cierto direccion"

## Correct

Direction (p. 18):

- El a biaha pa Miami.
- Nos a core den trein di Amsterdam pa Den Haag.
- Nan a muda pa otro ciudad.
- E hobennan a tumba pa lama.
- Despues di e accidente e auto a keda cara paseit.

Abstract or figurative, marked (a) on p. 19:

- Bo ta blo manda mi di cashi pa muraya.
- Bo rapport a bay di malo pa pio.
- Mi ta spera cu bo bishita n’ ta pa malo.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- Two examples from p. 18 are left out: no. 4 uses mucho for "a lot", which `_meta/invariants.md` replaces with hopi, and no. 7 is printed with "MI" in capitals.
- Point b of the same relation (the destinatario) is RULE_PRE_PA_BENEFICIARY_001. §1.3.8 lists eight relations for pa: espacio, tempo, causa, agente, intercambio, comparacion, frecuencia and secuencia.
- The rule pack cited pp. 28-29; those are PDF pages. Printed page = PDF page - 10 (see sources/cards/PRE2016.md).

## Rule

pa can mark the specific span of time within which an action or situation repeats itself.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.8 Pa, VII. Relacion di frecuencia, printed pp. 20-21.
"Den e relacion aki pa ta indica un lapso di tempo specifico den cua e accion of situacion ta ripiti su mes"

## Correct

- Sandro ta huma un pak’i sigaria pa dia.
- Nos tin les di Papiamento cuatro biaha pa siman.
- Su salario pa luna ta 3500 florin.
- Pedro ta bay tres biaha pa aña cu vakantie.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- cuatro is accepted next to cuater (`_meta/invariants.md`, "Words that must always be right").
- The rule pack cited pp. 29-31; those are PDF pages. Printed page = PDF page - 10 (see sources/cards/PRE2016.md).

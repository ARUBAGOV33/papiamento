## Rule

pa can mark the reason or motive why something happens.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.8 Pa, III. Relacion di causa, printed p. 19.
"Den e relacion aki pa ta indica e rason of motibo pakico algo ta sosode"

## Correct

- Hopi turista gusta Aruba pa su bunita beachnan.
- Pa su conocemento nan a vota p’e.
- Aloe ta hopi uza pa su beneficionan.
- Bo ta haya bo den problema pa bo mal cabes.
- Henter comunidad a yuda busca e mucha pa duele di e mama.
- El a yuda nos pa su propio interes.
- M’a keda babuca pa su conducta.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- p’e is pa + e; AB 2018 §10.1 point 2 (p. 33) says pa followed by the pronoun e is always written p’e.
- The rule pack cited pp. 28-29; those are PDF pages. Printed page = PDF page - 10 (see sources/cards/PRE2016.md).

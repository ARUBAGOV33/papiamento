## Rule

In its time sense, na marks a point in time or a certain period.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.7 Na, II. Relacion di tempo, printed p. 15.
"Uzo di na den un relacion temporal ta indica un punto den tempo of cierto periodo"

## Correct

- La Reina lo bishita nos na aña 2010.
- Cannen gusta biaha na juli.
- Nan dos a sali na e mesun momento.
- Na cuminsamento di aña tur hende ta bashi.
- Corda bin busca mi na ora.
- Sr. de Cuba ta na warda.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- "Cannen" is printed that way on the page (checked on a zoomed scan); it may be a typo for Carmen, but it is kept verbatim.

## Rule

In its spatial sense, entre marks a space in between two places or entities. In this sense entre is a synonym of memey di, and it can also be used in an abstract or metaphorical way.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.6 Entre, I. Relacion di espacio, printed p. 12, with Nota 1 on p. 13.
"Den e relacion aki entre ta indica un espacio intermedio" (p. 12); "Den e relacion aki entre ta sinonimo di memey di." (p. 13)

## Correct

Spatial (p. 12):

- Savaneta ta keda entre Pos Chikito y Cura Cabay.
- Den e teatro mi a sinta entre Isabel y Moira.
- E dede di memey ta keda entre e dede di renchi y e dede di mustra.
- E bruid tabata para entre su miho amiganan.

Abstract or metaphorical (p. 13, Nota 1):

- Su welo ta hopi malo; e ta lucha entre bida y morto.
- Religion ta siña nos scoge entre bon y malo.
- Entre teoria y practica tin un gran diferencia.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- §1.3.6 gives entre five senses: space, time, reciprocity, state and comparison. The others are RULE_PRE_ENTRE_TIME_001, RULE_PRE_ENTRE_RECIPROCITY_001, RULE_PRE_ENTRE_STATE_001 and RULE_PRE_ENTRE_COMPARISON_001.
- Notas 2 to 4 (p. 14) apply to every sense: entre stands before a coordination of two elements or before a plural noun, and two coordinated elements are joined with y (or cu), not pa. The pa rule is RULE_PRE_ENTRE_001.

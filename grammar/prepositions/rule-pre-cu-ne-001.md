## Rule
When the preposition cu is used with the personal pronoun e, a nasal /n/ is put between them, giving cu ne.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.3 Cu, Nota after VI. Preposicion fiho, printed page 6.
"Ora cu ta uza e preposicion cu hunto cu e pronomber personal e, ta introduci e zonido nasal /n/ memey di nan"

## Correct
- Pakico bo no ta bay cu ne?
- Ami cu ne ta bon amigo.

## Notes
- Only the pronoun e takes the /n/. When e is the article, cu e stays as it is, e.g. "E ladron a baha na awa cu e caha di placa." (p. 4). A checker must tell the pronoun from the article before flagging cu e.
- The page prints no incorrect sentence.

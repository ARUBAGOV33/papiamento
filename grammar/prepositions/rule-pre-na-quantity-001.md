## Rule

na can mark a definite or indefinite quantity. Under the same heading the book adds that na also marks a recipient or goal (destinatario of meta); this use has an abstract or figurative side too.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.7 Na, V. Relacion di cantidad, printed pp. 16-17.
"Den e relacion aki na ta indica un cantidad defini of indefini" (p. 16); "Na ta indica tambe destinatario of meta" (p. 17)

## Correct

Quantity (p. 17):

- Mi ta lag’e na 75 florin pa bo.
- Ta na dozijn so bo por cumpra su mercancia.
- Na e lavanderia tabatin paña na monton pa laba.
- E hobennan a yega na boshi na e exposicion di auto.
- Bo tin fout na granel den bo texto.
- Despues di e inundacion nan a bende nan cas na prijs di baca flaco.
- Ta miho bo desea tur cos na midi y no desea di mas.

Recipient or goal (p. 17):

- Na ken mi mester duna e regalo?
- Bo por duna e pañanan ey na e pobernan.
- El a entrega su carta di retiro na su director.

Recipient or goal, abstract or figurative (p. 17):

- Nan a tene un recepcion na su honor.
- No ta tur Arubiano ta traha na interes di nan pais.
- Den nos bario tin hopi cas grandi na benta.
- Hues a sentencia e asesino na morto.

## Notes

- Describes meanings; nothing for the checker to flag. A tagger would need to tell the quantity reading from the recipient reading by context.
- The rule pack called the first reading "price/amount". The page speaks of quantity in general; a price (75 florin) is only one of its examples.
- The introduction to §1.3.7 (p. 15) lists destinacion as its own relation, but the book gives it no heading of its own; it sits under V. Relacion di cantidad.
- Related: pa for a destinatario (RULE_PRE_PA_BENEFICIARY_001) and pa for an exchange value (RULE_PRE_PA_EXCHANGE_001); di for quantity (RULE_PRE_DI_QUANTITY_001).
- The rule pack cited pp. 26-27; those are PDF pages. Printed page = PDF page - 10 (see sources/cards/PRE2016.md).

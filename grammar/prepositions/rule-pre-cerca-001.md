## Rule
cerca can mark a relation of space or a characteristic. In space it gives a specific place, and with a verb of motion a specific destination. It can also mark a phenomenon or characteristic belonging to a person or group of persons.

## Source
Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento, 1.3.1 Cerca, printed pages 2–3.
"E preposicion cerca por denota un relacion di espacio of un caracteristica."

## Correct
- Federico ta biba cerca su tata.
- Por haya mas informacion cerca Sra. Comenencia na telefon 555-5555.
- Mi yiu, no yora mas, bin cerca tata.
- Ayera nos a bay cerca nos amigonan na Hotel Buena Vista.
- Cerca e Romanonan tabatin hopi custumber pagano.
- Hero tabata algo desconoci cerca e Indjannan Caquetio.

## Notes
- The old rule pack called the spatial sense "proximity"; the page speaks of a specific place or destination.
- The compound preposition cerca di is only listed on p. 1; this rule covers the simple preposition cerca.
- Describes uses only; nothing to flag.

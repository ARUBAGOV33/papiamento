## Rule

pa can mark the successive repetition of the same action.

## Source

Preposicion y Interheccion di Papiamento (PRE2016), §1.3.8 Pa, VIII. Relacion di secuencia, printed p. 21.
"Den e relacion aki pa ta indica ripiticion sucesivo di e mesun accion"

## Correct

- Un pa un nan a sali bay.
- E baby a pasa di man pa man.
- Sonia a ripiti e storia palabra pa palabra.
- E noticia a core lihe di boca pa boca.
- Dia pa dia criminalidad ta aumenta riba nos isla.

## Notes

- Describes a meaning; nothing for the checker to flag.
- In all five examples the same word stands on both sides of pa. A checker should leave these pairs alone.
- The rule pack cited pp. 29-31; those are PDF pages. Printed page = PDF page - 10 (see sources/cards/PRE2016.md).

## Rule

An adjective qualifies or specifies a noun, a pronoun or a phrase. It has two uses: attributive and predicative.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §3.1 "Definicion", p. 15: "Adhetivo ta califica of specifica un sustantivo, pronomber of frase". §3.3 "Uzo", p. 17: "Tin dos uzo di adhetivo: uzo atributivo y uzo predicativo."

## Correct

- Barack Obama ta un presidente serio y sincero.
- Tur su dos yiu grandinan tin un posicion responsabel den gobierno.
- E fiesta cu Grupo Cristal a organisa pa su miembronan tabata tremendo.
- E cas aki ta nobo, pero esaya ta bieu.
- Tur auto kibra mester ta hala for di caminda.

## Notes

- Attributive use: RULE_MOR_ADJ_ATTR_001 to RULE_MOR_ADJ_ATTR_004. Predicative use: RULE_MOR_ADJ_PRED_001.
- "Tur su dos yiu grandinan" puts nan on the adjective after a possessive and numeral, as RULE_MOR_NOUN_PLURAL_003 allows.
- A classification rule; there is nothing for the checker to flag.

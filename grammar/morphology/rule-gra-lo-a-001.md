## Rule

By maintainer ruling, the future particle lo and the past particle a are never combined. Use lo alone for the future or a alone for the past.

## Source

Maintainer ruling in _meta/invariants.md, Grammar: "lo (future) and a (past) never combine. Use one or the other." No source book states this rule.

## Correct

- mi a bay (from _meta/invariants.md)

## Incorrect

- lo a bay → mi a bay (the ruling's own pair in _meta/invariants.md)

## Notes

- **Conflict decided: the maintainer kept the ruling on 2026-10-17** (sources/errata.tsv err-0019 to err-0022). Both Departamento di Enseñansa grammars treat lo a as a compound particle:
  - err-0019: Morfologia §6.3.1 (p. 42) lists "Componi: lo a – lo tabata – lo ta".
  - err-0020: §6.3.1.2 Particula componi (pp. 43–44) gives, under "Particula lo a": "Pa ora bo yega, ya lo mi a come caba."
  - err-0021: Morfologia p. 57 (reflexive pronoun example): "Mi no por a imagina mi (mes) cu e asunto aki lo a caba asina."
  - err-0022: Sintaxis §8.3.1.4 Uzo di tempo y aspecto, II. Futuro perfectivo (p. 119): "Den futuro perfectivo ta uza e combinacion lo a + verbo pa expresa modalidad", with "Si nan lo a ofrece mi e trabou, lo mi a acepta."
- The decision did not change the scope: RULE_GRA_LO_A_001 flags only lo and a written next to each other, not lo + subject + a as in most of the book examples.
- The old rule pack's trigger was the adjacent string "lo a".

## Rule

The gerund is used to stress that an action is taking place at the moment of speaking, or was taking place at the moment the event of the main verb was happening.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.2.3 Uzo, p. 48: "Ta uza gerundio pa enfatisa cu un accion ta tumando luga na e momento cu e papiado ta papiando". §6.6.2 (p. 47) defines the gerund as the form that "ta expresa un accion of un proceso den progreso".

## Correct

- Tecla no por bini, e ta bañando!
- Net ora e tabata pasando eybanda, Nanzi a mira e mamanan ta yena saco cu bonchi.

## Notes

- The page does not make the gerund obligatory: the infinitive can be used instead with the same meaning (p. 47); see RULE_MOR_GERUND_006. Nothing is flagged by this rule.
- Stilistica §9 Frase cu gerundio (PDF page 4) adds style advice on gerund clauses; see RULE_STI_GER_001.

## Rule

Adjectives can be derived from other adjectives.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §3.2.1.3 "Adhetivo deriva" (p. 15) and its part II "Adhetivo deriva di adhetivo", p. 16.

## Correct

- grandi - grandioso
- agradabel - desagradabel
- chulo - chulastico

## Notes

- The page lists pairs only; it gives no rule for which suffix or prefix to use.
- Related: RULE_MOR_ADJ_DERIVE_001 (from nouns), RULE_MOR_ADJ_DERIVE_003 (from verbs). Diminutive adjectives (pobercito, chikitito, flakito) are in Nota 1 on the same page.
- A word-formation rule; there is nothing for the checker to flag.

## Rule

There are two kinds of serial verb. A simultaneous serial verb expresses different actions or processes at the same moment, as if they were interlocked; a consecutive serial verb expresses different actions that follow one another. It is sometimes hard to decide which kind a serial verb is.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §6.5.1 "Subcategoria di verbo serial" and its Nota, p. 46: "Tin dos sorto di verbo serial: verbo serial simultaneo y verbo serial consecutivo."

## Correct

- Simultaneous (examples 1, 3 and 5 of §6.5): Lulu ta sinta lesa den porch tur mainta.
- Simultaneous: Diripiente e mucha a lanta bula for di su stoel.
- Simultaneous: Prome el a cana discuti cu su yiu y djey el a bras’e, sunch’e.
- Consecutive (examples 2 and 4): Chico a drenta camber, coy un buki, sali bay.
- Consecutive: Mi mama a lora un pan, duna mi e, manda mi bay scol.

## Notes

- The Nota names sali bay (example 2), lanta bula (example 3) and bras’e, sunch’e (example 5) as cases that may raise doubt about whether they are simultaneous or consecutive. Any label a tool gives these should be treated as uncertain.

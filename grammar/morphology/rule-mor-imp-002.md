## Rule

Three verbs have an irregular imperative: bay becomes ban (with nos), ta becomes ser or sea, and tin becomes tene.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.4.1.2 Imperativo iregular, p. 45: "Tin algun forma iregular di Imperativo."

## Correct

- bay – ban (nos); ta – ser of sea; tin – tene.
- Laga nos bay/ban cas!

## Notes

- Sintaxis, Nota on p. 120, lists the same three verbs with the examples "Ban cas.", "Sea amabel." and "Tene compasion."
- The page prints "Laga nos bay/ban cas!" with both forms, so a checker must not flag bay after laga nos.
- The book prints no incorrect imperative. Severity follows the old rule pack (error); a reviewer may lower it.

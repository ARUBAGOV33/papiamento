## Rule

By form, Morfologia sorts common nouns into simple, compound and derived nouns. A simple noun consists of a single word.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §2.2.2.1 "Subclasificacion segun forma" (p. 7) and its part I "Sustantivo simpel", p. 8: "E sustantivonan aki ta consisti di un palabra so".

## Correct

- cashi, sapato, curason, strea, siman, periodo, tecnologia, arkitectura, simpatia, sociologia, banki, borchi

## Notes

- The Nota on p. 8 says some words have one form that works as a simple noun or as an adjective (ciego, rico, pober, hoben, bieu, adulto, adolescente, bobo, santo, sokete); the sentence shows which. Its examples: "E riconan y e pobernan tin un bida hopi diferente (sustantivo)." and "Hende rico y hende pober tin un bida hopi diferente (adhetivo)."
- Related: RULE_MOR_NOUN_COMPOUND_001, RULE_MOR_NOUN_DERIVED_001. Simple adjectives: §3.2.1.1, p. 15.
- A classification rule; there is nothing for the checker to flag. The old rule pack's lexicon instruction (store as base form) is not in the book and was dropped.

## Rule

An adjective is placed before the noun when it specifies the quality of the noun emphatically.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §3.3.1.2 "Adhetivo dilanti di sustantivo", case a, pp. 17-18: "ta specifica e calidad di e sustantivo enfaticamente".

## Correct

- Chef Toribio a traha un dushi sopi pa mi fiesta.
- Durante e concierto Mientje su bunita bos a resalta.
- E pareha Hassel a traha un tremendo cas na Hofi Santa Lucia.

## Notes

- Default position after the noun: RULE_MOR_ADJ_ATTR_001. The same emphatic fronting in predicative use: RULE_MOR_ADJ_PRED_001 (§3.3.2.2, p. 19).
- With adjective + noun, nan goes only after the noun: RULE_MOR_NOUN_PLURAL_006.

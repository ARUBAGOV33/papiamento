## Rule

When the infinitive is used outside the predicate, it is generally the subject of the sentence or part of the subject.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.1 Infinitivo, pp. 46–47: "Generalmente ora uza infinitivo pafo di e predicado e ta suheto of parti di e suheto di e frase".

## Correct

- Come ta necesario pa sigui na bida.
- Huma ta masha dañino pa salud.
- Haci deporte ta tene e curpa den condicion.

## Notes

- A checker should not flag a bare infinitive in subject position; the book treats it as normal.
- Sintaxis §2.3.2.3 (Verbo infinitivo como suheto, p. 37) covers the same use. Its Nota on p. 38 adds that the infinitive subject can be replaced by the noun derived from it ("humamento en bes di huma").

## Rule

When the i of iendo comes between two vowels, it changes to y.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.2.1 Formacion, Nota, p. 48: "Ora cu e i di iendo aparece entre dos vocal, e ta cambia pa y".

## Correct

- construyendo, contribuyendo, destruyendo, instruyendo etc.

## Notes

- The page prints no incorrect form. Severity is error because it is a spelling rule stated without qualification: a gerund spelled with a vowel + iendo where this rule applies is a misspelling.
- The AB 2018 word list has construyendo (word list p. 16).
- Related: RULE_MOR_GERUND_002 (the -iendo ending).

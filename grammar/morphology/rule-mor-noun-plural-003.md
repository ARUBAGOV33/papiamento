## Rule

When an adjective follows the noun, nan can go in either of two places: after the noun or after the adjective. Both positions are correct.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §2.3.4.1 "Posicion di e sufiho nan cu sustantivo + adhetivo", p. 11: "Den e combinacion aki e sufiho nan por tin dos posicion". Repeated in §3.2.1.3 Nota 2, p. 16.

## Correct

- e casnan grandi / e cas grandinan
- e flornan bunita / e flor bunitanan
- nos yiunan chikito / nos yiu chikitonan
- e barbuletnan cora / e barbulet coranan
- e streanan famoso / e strea famosonan
- Su dos rumannan grandi ta bay biba na Parkietenbos. (p. 16)
- Su dos ruman grandinan ta bay biba na Parkietenbos. (p. 16)

## Exceptions

- With more than one adjective after the noun, nan goes only after the noun (Nota, pp. 11-12): RULE_MOR_NOUN_PLURAL_005.

## Notes

- This file also covers RULE_MOR_NOUN_PLURAL_004 (nan after the adjective). The two old rules are the two halves of the same statement on p. 11, so they are written as one rule.
- The old rule pack warned against putting nan on both the noun and the adjective; the page does not say that, so it is not stated here.
- "Su dos rumannan" has nan after a numeral because the possessive su is there; this matches the invariant (_meta/invariants.md).

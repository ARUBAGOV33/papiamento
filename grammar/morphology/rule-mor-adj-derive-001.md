## Rule

Adjectives can be derived from nouns.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §3.2.1.3 "Adhetivo deriva" (p. 15: "Ta deriva adhetivo di sustantivo, adhetivo y verbo.") and its part I "Adhetivo deriva di sustantivo", p. 16.

## Correct

- nervio - nervioso
- cautela - cauteloso
- teatro - teatral

## Notes

- The page lists pairs only; it gives no rule for which suffix to use.
- Related: RULE_MOR_ADJ_DERIVE_002 (from adjectives), RULE_MOR_ADJ_DERIVE_003 (from verbs).
- A word-formation rule; there is nothing for the checker to flag.

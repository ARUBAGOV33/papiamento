## Rule

The participle is the verb form that is used as an adjective or to form the passive voice.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.3 Participio, p. 48: "Participio ta e forma di verbo cu ta uza como adhetivo of pa forma bos pasivo".

## Correct

- Un amor fracasa ta un amor lubida.
- E tentamen ta traha caba.
- Tur e frutanan a wordo exporta pa Aruba.

## Notes

- Formation of the participle (§6.6.3.1, pp. 48–49): RULE_MOR_PART_002 to RULE_MOR_PART_005. Rule 5 of that section (ge- + infinitive for verbs from other languages than Spanish or ending in a consonant: gedal, geverf) and the irregular participles of §6.6.3.2 (kier – keri; sa – sabi), both on p. 49, have no rule file in this batch.
- The passive voice is described in §6.7 Bos activo y bos pasivo (p. 50).

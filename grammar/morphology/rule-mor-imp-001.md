## Rule

Normally the imperative has the same form as the infinitive. A few verbs (bay, ta and tin) have an irregular imperative.

## Source

Sintaxis, §8.3.1.5 Uzo di modo imperativo, Nota, p. 120: "Normalmente na Papiamento e forma imperativo ta igual cu infinitivo p.e. pasa, subi, biba."

## Correct

- pasa, subi, biba
- Habri bo boca un biaha mas, anto bo ta mira.

## Exceptions

- bay, ta and tin have an irregular imperative (same Nota, p. 120); see RULE_MOR_IMP_002.

## Notes

- Morfologia §6.4.1.1 Formacion (pp. 44–45) describes how the imperative is made: by a change of tone in two-syllable verbs that have low–high tone in the infinitive and are followed by a pause (come > Come!), or with the verb laga (Laga mi bisa bo cuant’or tin!). In those examples the verb is spelled the same as the infinitive; only the tone marking in brackets differs.

## Rule

The verb sa (sabi) has the irregular gerund sabiendo.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.2.2 Gerundio iregular, p. 48: "sa(bi) – sabiendo".

## Correct

- sa(bi) – sabiendo

## Notes

- The page prints no wrong form. The wrong form sabindo → sabiendo is in lexicon/barbarismos (bar-0040) and in _meta/invariants.md (Aruban irregular gerunds).
- The AB 2018 word list has sabiendo (word list p. 59).
- sa is a modal and takes no ta (_meta/invariants.md); this file only gives its gerund form, as the page does.
- Part of the full list in RULE_MOR_GERUND_004; kept as its own file because bar-0040 links to this id.

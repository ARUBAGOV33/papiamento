## Rule

A serial verb is a series of verbs that follow one another in a sentence, with a single particle and a single subject. The first verb of the series always has the subject and takes the particle.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §6.5 "Verbo serial", pp. 45-46: "Verbo serial ta un serie di verbo cu ta sigui otro den un frase, cu un solo particula y cu un solo suheto."

## Correct

- Lulu ta sinta lesa den porch tur mainta.
- Chico a drenta camber, coy un buki, sali bay.
- Diripiente e mucha a lanta bula for di su stoel.
- Mi mama a lora un pan, duna mi e, manda mi bay scol.
- Prome el a cana discuti cu su yiu y djey el a bras’e, sunch’e.

## Notes

- In the last example the particle a appears twice because there are two series, each with its own subject el.
- The PDF text layer has a straight apostrophe in "bras'e, sunch'e" in example 5; the printed glyph is the same curly apostrophe as in the Nota below it, so ’ is used here.

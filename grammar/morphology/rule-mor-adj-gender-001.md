## Rule

Papiamento has no grammatical gender, but some adjectives have a feminine form borrowed from Spanish.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §3.3.1.3, Nota 2, p. 18: "Papiamento no conoce genero" and "tin algun adhetivo cu tin forma femenino fia di Spaño".

## Correct

- Semana Santa
- buena fe
- Iglesia Catolica
- mala suerte

## Notes

- The page gives these as examples ("algun adhetivo"), not as a closed list.
- The page prints no incorrect forms, so this rule gives the checker nothing specific to flag. The old rule pack's advice not to inflect adjectives for gender follows from the statement but is not printed.

## Rule

The modal verb por has no gerund in Aruban Papiamento. Do not write podiendo or porndo; rephrase the sentence without the progressive.

## Source

Maintainer ruling: sources/errata.tsv err-0010 and _meta/invariants.md, Grammar ("por and prefera have no gerund"). No source book states this rule; Morfologia §6.6.2.2 (p. 48) prints the opposite and was decided against.

## Incorrect

- podiendo → no gerund; rephrase without the progressive (Morfologia p. 48 prints "por – podiendo"; overruled by err-0010)
- porndo → no gerund; rephrase without the progressive (listed in _meta/invariants.md)

## Notes

- This file also covers RULE_MOR_GERUND_PORNDO (trigger porndo), which states the same rule and was not written as a separate file.
- The AB 2018 word list has no podiendo.
- The old rule pack suggested ta por as a rewrite. That is not repeated here: _meta/invariants.md rules that modals never take ta (see RULE_GRA_MODAL_TA_001).
- The old rule pack called podiendo a Curaçao/Spanish form; no source page says so, so that claim is dropped.

## Rule

The infinitive of the verb can be used instead of the gerund without changing the meaning of the sentence.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.2 Gerundio, Nota, p. 47: "Por uza e forma infinitivo di e verbo en bes di gerundio, cu e mesun nificacion di e frase".

## Correct

- Cada dia nos ta perdiendo mas placa pa via di inflacion.
- Cada dia nos ta perde mas placa pa via di inflacion.

## Notes

- A checker must not flag ta + infinitive as a missing gerund.
- Related: RULE_MOR_GERUND_005 (use of the gerund).

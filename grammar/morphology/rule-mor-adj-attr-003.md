## Rule

An adjective is placed before the noun when the phrase is a form of address (apelativo).

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §3.3.1.2 "Adhetivo dilanti di sustantivo", case b, p. 18: "ta trata di un apelativo".

## Correct

- Reverendo Mgr. Wim Ellis tabata conoci pa su remarcanan humoristico.
- Apreciabel Señora Solognier ...
- Honorabel Miembronan di Parlamento ...

## Notes

- Default position after the noun: RULE_MOR_ADJ_ATTR_001.
- In "Honorabel Miembronan" nan follows the noun, as RULE_MOR_NOUN_PLURAL_006 requires for adjective + noun.

## Rule

The infinitive is the verb form that names what the verb expresses. It is the form used together with the particle to form the predicate.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.1 Infinitivo, p. 46: "Infinitivo ta e forma cu ta duna nomber di loke e verbo ta expresa."

## Correct

- tuma, come, pidi, fango, pupu, dal, por, stop, verf etc.

## Notes

- §6.6 (same page) names the three verb forms of Papiamento: "infinitivo, gerundio y participio".
- The old rule pack used this as a lexicon convention (store verb headwords in the infinitive). The page itself only says what the infinitive is and how it is used; nothing here is flagged.

## Rule

If a verb has two syllables and ends in a or i, the participle is formed by moving the stress from the first syllable to the last.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.3.1 Formacion, rule 1, pp. 48–49: "Si e verbo tin dos silaba y e ta caba cu vocal a of vocal i, ta cambia acentuacion di e prome silaba pa e delaster silaba".

## Correct

- [pá-sa] → [pa-sá] Hopi aña pasa mi a bay Grecia.
- [há-bri] → [ha-brí] E porta a wordo habri pa e mucha drenta.

## Notes

- The change is in pronunciation only: the written participle is spelled like the infinitive (pasa, habri). The accent marks appear only inside the bracketed pronunciation notation; Aruban spelling uses no accent marks (_meta/invariants.md). Nothing is flagged in writing.
- Related: RULE_MOR_PART_003 (two-syllable verbs ending in e).

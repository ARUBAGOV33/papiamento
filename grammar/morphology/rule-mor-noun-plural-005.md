## Rule

When more than one adjective follows a noun with a plural meaning, nan is placed only after the noun.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §2.3.4.1, Nota, pp. 11-12: "Ora tin mas cu un adhetivo tras di un sustantivo cu un nificacion plural, ta pone e sufiho nan solamente tras di e sustantivo".

## Correct

- Nan a bende e casnan grandi, bunita y barata cu algun stranhero.
- Nos no ta kere e noticianan sensacional y provocante di e corant ey.
- Pakico nan no a contrata e futbolistanan stranhero excelente ey?

## Notes

- This is the exception to RULE_MOR_NOUN_PLURAL_003, where a single adjective may carry nan instead of the noun.
- The page prints no incorrect forms. A checker would flag nan on an adjective when two or more adjectives follow the noun.

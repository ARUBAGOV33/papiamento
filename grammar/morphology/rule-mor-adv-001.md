## Rule

Adverbs are closely related to adjectives: some adverbs are derived from adjectives, some have the form of an adjective, and some have degrees of comparison. Adjectives are frequently used as adverbs, in the same form.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §5.3.1 "Adverbio y adhetivo", p. 31: "Adverbio tin un relacion estrecho cu adhetivo". §5.3.1.2 "Adverbio cu forma di adhetivo", p. 33: "Frecuentemente ta uza adhetivo como adverbio".

## Correct

- barata, bunita, caro, duro, feliz, lihe, mahos, moli, pocopoco
- Adjective: Mi ruman tin un cas bunita. / Adverb: Mi ruman a verf su cas bunita.
- Adjective: Nan ta un pareha feliz. / Adverb: E pareha ey ta biba feliz cu otro.
- Adjective: Un atleta semper ta lihe. / Adverb: E atletanan ta core masha lihe.

## Notes

- Derivation with the suffix mente (§5.3.1.1, pp. 31-33) is not restated here and could be its own rule. Two points there a reviewer may want as rules: not every adjective yields an adverb (Nota, p. 32), and the book recommends the regular unanimemente over the variant unanimamente (p. 32).
- The old rule pack's instruction to tag adverbs and adjectives separately in the lexicon is not in the book. The point the page supports is that one form can work as either, so the function shows only in the sentence.
- A classification rule; there is nothing for the checker to flag.

## Rule

Morfologia distinguishes eight kinds of pronoun: personal, possessive, reflexive, reciprocal, demonstrative, relative, interrogative and indefinite.

## Source

Morfologia, §7.2 Clasificacion y uzo, p. 53: "Ta distingui e siguiente pronombernan: pronomber personal, pronomber posesivo, pronomber reflexivo, pronomber reciproco, pronomber demostrativo, pronomber relativo, pronomber interogativo y pronomber indefini."

## Correct

- Ken tin cinco florin?
- Nos alumnonan ta siña con pa yuda nan mes.
- Cada ken busca nan caminda.

## Notes

- The examples are from §7.1 Definicion (same page), which says a pronoun replaces a noun.
- Each kind has its own subsection (§7.2.1 to §7.2.8, from p. 53). The personal pronoun table is on p. 53, the possessive table on p. 55, the reflexive table on p. 57.
- The old rule pack used this as a lexicon convention (store the pronoun subclass); nothing is flagged.

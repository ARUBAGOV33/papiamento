## Rule

A compound noun consists of more than one word. Its usual structure is noun + the preposition di + noun.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §2.2.2.1 II "Sustantivo componi" and its Nota, p. 8: "Sustantivo componi ta consisti di mas cu un palabra".

## Correct

- palo di tamarein, awa di wowo, cos di hunga, mucha homber, palomba gay, mashin di cose
- Noun + di + noun: cas di palomba, neishi di maribomba, muhe di bida, palo di awa, diario di nabegacion, paña di rouw

## Notes

- The Nota says "Por lo general" (as a rule), so noun + di + noun is the usual pattern, not the only one: mucha homber and palomba gay in the first list have no di.
- Where nan goes in a plural compound noun: RULE_MOR_NOUN_PLURAL_007 (§2.3.4.3, p. 12).
- A classification rule; there is nothing for the checker to flag.

## Rule

Some verbs have an irregular gerund: kier – keriendo, sa(bi) – sabiendo, ta – siendo, estando, and tin – teniendo. The book also lists drumi – durmiendo, por – podiendo and prefera – prefiriendo; by maintainer ruling the gerund of drumi is drumiendo, and por and prefera have no gerund.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.2.2 Gerundio iregular, p. 48: "Tin algun forma iregular di gerundio."

## Correct

- kier – keriendo
- sa(bi) – sabiendo
- ta – siendo, estando
- tin – teniendo

## Incorrect

- durmiendo → drumiendo (listed on this page as the gerund of drumi; overruled by err-0009)
- podiendo → no gerund; rephrase without the progressive (listed on this page as the gerund of por; overruled by err-0010)
- prefiriendo → no gerund; rephrase without the progressive (listed on this page as the gerund of prefera; overruled by err-0011)

## Notes

- Rulings: sources/errata.tsv err-0009, err-0010, err-0011; _meta/invariants.md, Grammar ("por and prefera have no gerund"; "Aruban irregular gerunds").
- The AB 2018 word list has drumiendo (p. 23), keriendo (p. 39), sabiendo (p. 59), siendo (p. 61) and teniendo (p. 66). It has no durmiendo, podiendo, prefiriendo or estando. estando is printed on this page but is not in the word list; no ruling covers it yet.
- drumiendo is also what the regular -iendo rule gives for drumi (RULE_MOR_GERUND_002).

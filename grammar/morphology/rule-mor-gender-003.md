## Rule

For persons, the distinction is made with a different word or by adding a word; the added words are mostly homber and muhe. For animals, a different word is also used, or the distinction is made with homber and muhe or with macho and embra.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §2.4.2 "Distincion cu un elemento lexical", p. 13: "Ora agrega palabra pa haci e distincion ta uza mas tanto e sustantivonan homber y muhe".

## Correct

- Persons: sua - cuña, homber - muhe, omo - tanta, yiu homber - yiu muhe
- Animals: toro - baca, cabay - yewa, pushi macho (homber) - pushi embra (muhe)

## Rule

Numerals are definite, indicating an exact quantity, or indefinite, indicating an inexact quantity. Definite numerals are cardinals, ordinals, fractions and multiples; indefinite numerals are cardinals, ordinals and multiples.

## Source

Morfologia, §9.3 Clasificacion y uzo, p. 67: "Ta clasifica numeral den e subcategorianan defini y indefini."

## Correct

From the table on p. 67:

- Definite cardinal: un, cuater, nuebe, cincuentiseis, dies mil
- Indefinite cardinal: hopi, varios, tur, diferente
- Definite ordinal: e di dos, e di seis, e di shete
- Indefinite ordinal: di tanto, di tantisimo, penultimo, ultimo
- Fraction: dos di tres (parti)
- Definite multiple: dobel, tripel, cuadrupel
- Indefinite multiple: multipel

## Notes

- §9.2 Forma (same page): numerals are simple (dos, tur) or compound (diescuater, e di cinco).
- How numbers are written (11–99 as one word, above 100 separately) is in AB 2018 §11.8 (p. 38) and _meta/invariants.md.
- A noun combined with a numeral takes no plural nan unless it also has the article e, a possessive or a demonstrative (Morfologia §2.3.1 c and §2.3.2, pp. 10–11); see RULE_GRA_PLURAL_001.
- The table prints "nuebe" and "cincuentiseis" on two lines of one cell with no comma between them; they are two separate examples.
- The old rule pack used this as a lexicon convention (tag numeral features); nothing is flagged.

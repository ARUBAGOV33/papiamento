## Rule

If a verb has more than two syllables and ends in a or i, the participle stays the same as the infinitive.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.3.1 Formacion, rule 3, p. 49: "Si e verbo tin mas di dos silaba y e ta caba cu vocal a of vocal i, e participio ta keda mescos cu e infinitivo".

## Correct

- [pa-ke-tá] → [pa-ke-tá] E regalo tabata bunita paketa.
- [dis-cu-tí] → [dis-cu-tí] E asunto a wordo discuti te na final.

## Notes

- The bracketed notation shows that the stress does not move either; the written form is the infinitive (paketa, discuti). Nothing is flagged.
- Related: RULE_MOR_PART_005 (longer verbs ending in e).

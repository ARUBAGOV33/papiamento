## Rule

Papiamento generally makes no grammatical distinction between masculine and feminine nouns. Only nouns that refer to a person or an animal make a distinction, a semantic one, made with a morphological element or with a lexical element.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §2.4 "Genero", p. 12: "Papiamento por lo general no ta haci distincion gramatical entre sustantivo masculino y sustantivo femenino."

## Rule

Used predicatively, an adjective qualifies or specifies a noun, pronoun or phrase and forms part of a nominal predicate. In general it stands after the noun, pronoun or phrase; it stands before it when the quality is specified emphatically.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §3.3.2 "Uzo predicativo", §3.3.2.1 "Adhetivo patras di sustantivo, pronomber of frase" and §3.3.2.2 "Adhetivo dilanti di sustantivo, pronomber of frase", p. 19.

## Correct

- After: Aruba su hendenan ta hospitalario.
- After: Bo no ta mustra dje malo ey.
- After: Loke bo a bisa ta increibel.
- Before, emphatic: Grandi tabata mi sorpresa ora mi a tende e noticia.
- Before, emphatic: Loco bo ta?
- Before, emphatic: Ta increibel loke bo a bisa.

## Notes

- The page numbers the second and third examples of §3.3.2.1 both "2."; the sentences are copied as printed.
- Copulative verbs that form a nominal predicate (bira, keda, mustra, parce, sinti, ta, yama): §6.2.2.2, p. 38; see RULE_MOR_VERB_CLASS_001.
- Lead for a separate rule: §6.2.2.2 Nota 3 (p. 39) marks "*E ta logico cu cada pais tin un gobierno." as incorrect; the correct form is "Ta logico cu cada pais tin un gobierno." (p. 38).

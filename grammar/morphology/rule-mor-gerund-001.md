## Rule

The gerund is generally formed from verbs that end in a vowel and come from Spanish verbs. If the verb ends in a, the a is dropped and ando is added.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.2.1 Formacion, rule 1, p. 47: "Si e verbo ta caba cu vocal a, ta laga e vocal aki cay afo y ta pone ando acerca".

## Correct

- pasa → pas + ando → pasando
- hunga → hung + ando → hungando

## Exceptions

- The irregular gerunds in §6.6.2.2 (p. 48); see RULE_MOR_GERUND_004.

## Notes

- The page limits the rule with "Generalmente" and to verbs that come from Spanish, so this file describes formation and flags nothing by itself.
- prefera ends in a, but by maintainer ruling it has no gerund (err-0011), so preferando is not used; see RULE_MOR_GERUND_PREFIRIENDO.

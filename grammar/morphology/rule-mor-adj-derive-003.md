## Rule

Adjectives can be derived from verbs.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §3.2.1.3 "Adhetivo deriva" (p. 15) and its part III "Adhetivo deriva di verbo", p. 16.

## Correct

- discuti - discutibel
- dal - gedal
- yama - yamativo
- venga - vengativo

## Notes

- gedal is also the participle of dal (§6.6.3.1, p. 49), and participles are used as adjectives: "E auto gedal ta na benta." (§3.3.1.3 Nota 3, p. 19). Participles as adjectives: RULE_MOR_PART_001.
- Related: RULE_MOR_ADJ_DERIVE_001 (from nouns), RULE_MOR_ADJ_DERIVE_002 (from adjectives).
- A word-formation rule; there is nothing for the checker to flag.

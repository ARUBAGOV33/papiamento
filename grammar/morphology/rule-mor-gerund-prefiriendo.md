## Rule

The verb prefera has no gerund in Aruban Papiamento. Do not write prefiriendo or preferando; rephrase the sentence without the progressive.

## Source

Maintainer ruling: sources/errata.tsv err-0011 and _meta/invariants.md, Grammar ("por and prefera have no gerund"). No source book states this rule; Morfologia §6.6.2.2 (p. 48) prints the opposite and was decided against.

## Incorrect

- prefiriendo → no gerund; rephrase without the progressive (Morfologia p. 48 prints "prefera – prefiriendo"; overruled by err-0011)
- preferando → no gerund; rephrase without the progressive (listed in _meta/invariants.md; it is what the regular -ando rule, RULE_MOR_GERUND_001, would give)

## Notes

- This file also covers RULE_MOR_GERUND_PREFERANDO (trigger preferando), which states the same rule and was not written as a separate file.
- The old rule pack also flagged prefarando (RULE_MOR_GERUND_PREFARANDO). It is printed on no page, and the maintainer dropped it on 2026-10-17.
- The AB 2018 word list has no prefiriendo and no preferando.
- The ruling covers prefera only. The AB 2018 word list also prints preferi, preferibel and preferiblemente (word list p. 53; pap-011844 to pap-011846); the maintainer confirmed on 2026-10-17 that the ruling does not cover preferi.
- The old rule pack called prefiriendo a Curaçao/Spanish form; no source page says so, so that claim is dropped.
- Related: RULE_MOR_GERUND_004 (irregular gerunds), RULE_MOR_GERUND_PODIENDO.

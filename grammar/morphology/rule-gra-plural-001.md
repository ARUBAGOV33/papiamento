## Rule

A noun with plural meaning does not take the suffix nan when it is combined with a definite or indefinite numeral. The suffix nan is used when the noun has a definite article, a possessive pronoun or a demonstrative.

## Source

Morfologia, §2.3.1 Sustantivo cu nificacion plural sin e sufiho nan, case c, p. 10: "e sustantivo ta combina cu un numeral defini of un numeral indefini". The cases with nan are in §2.3.2, pp. 10–11.

## Correct

- Bo no ta contento ainda cu bo ta gana tres mil florin?
- Ayera nochi nos a mira hopi auto riba caretera.
- Tin diferente asunto pa discuti ainda riba agenda.

## Exceptions

- With the definite article e, a possessive pronoun or a demonstrative, nan is used (§2.3.2 a–c, pp. 10–11), also together with a numeral: "E tres edificionan aya ta di Gobierno." (p. 11)

## Notes

- Stilistica §12 Forma plural (PDF page 5; the document has no printed page numbers) gives the same general rule for a numeral or a group of words that indicates a quantity, with the same exception for e and possessives. Its example: "cinco buki".
- _meta/invariants.md: "No -nan after a bare numeral. With an article or possessive it is fine."
- The old rule pack's incorrect example (cinco bukinan → cinco buki) is not printed in either source, so it is not listed under Incorrect.

## Rule

If a verb has two syllables and ends in e, the participle changes the final e to i and moves the stress from the first syllable to the last.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.3.1 Formacion, rule 2, p. 49: "Si e verbo tin dos silaba y e ta caba cu vocal e, ta cambia e e final pa vocal i".

## Correct

- [có-me] → [co-mí] E cuminda a wordo bon comi.
- [pér-de] → [per-dí] E pober mucha ta completamente perdi caba.

## Notes

- Unlike rule 1, this changes the spelling: the participle is written comi, perdi. The accent marks are only in the bracketed pronunciation notation; the written forms have none (_meta/invariants.md).

## Rule

Used attributively, an adjective qualifies or specifies a noun and can stand after or before it. In general, the adjective is placed after the noun.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §3.3.1 "Uzo atributivo" and §3.3.1.1 "Adhetivo patras di sustantivo", p. 17: "En general ta uza adhetivo patras di un sustantivo".

## Correct

- Philomena Wong a skirbi diferente poesia feminista.
- Juancho Irausquin semper tabata tene discurso inspirado.
- Hubert (Lio) Booi ta conoci como un homber culto.

## Exceptions

- Before the noun when the quality is specified emphatically (§3.3.1.2 a, pp. 17-18): RULE_MOR_ADJ_ATTR_002.
- Before the noun in a form of address (§3.3.1.2 b, p. 18): RULE_MOR_ADJ_ATTR_003.
- Some adjectives change meaning with their position (§3.3.1.3, p. 18): RULE_MOR_ADJ_ATTR_004.

## Notes

- The old rule pack made this a warning and proposed moving fronted adjectives after the noun. The page does not call a fronted adjective wrong, so this is info and the checker should not flag adjective position.

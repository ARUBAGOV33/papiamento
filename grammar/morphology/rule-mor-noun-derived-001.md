## Rule

Derived nouns are generally formed with the suffixes mento, cion, sion, xion, do or dor, idad, edad and encia. Some derived nouns are formed with a prefix, such as ab, pre, re or in.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §2.2.2.1 III "Sustantivo deriva", pp. 8-9: "Por lo general e sustantivonan aki ta forma cu e sufihonan mento, cion, sion, xion, do of dor, idad, edad y encia".

## Correct

- With a suffix: comemento, sangramento, bistimento, suplamento, dedicacion, combersacion, salbacion, frustracion, pretension, comprension, conexion, pompado, hungado, trahado(r), gobernador, orador, felicidad, trankilidad, scarsedad, bobedad, violencia, presencia
- With a prefix: abnormalidad, absolucion, preseleccion, predestinacion, recapacitacion, restructuracion, indecencia, independencia

## Notes

- The -cion spelling matches the invariant that the /-shon/ suffix is written -cion (_meta/invariants.md).
- A word-formation rule; there is nothing for the checker to flag. The old rule pack's lexicon instruction (link each noun to its base) is not in the book and was dropped.

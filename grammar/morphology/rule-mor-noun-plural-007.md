## Rule

In a compound noun, nan can go in either of two places: after the first component or after the last component.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §2.3.4.3 "Posicion di e sufiho nan den sustantivo componi", p. 12: "Ora ta trata di e sustantivonan aki e sufiho nan por tin dos posicion".

## Correct

- e mashinnan di cose / e mashin di cosenan
- e palonan di tamarein / e pal’i tamareinnan
- e hendenan muhe / e hende muhenan
- e palombanan gay / e palomba gaynan

## Notes

- The PDF text layer reads "pal’ tamareinnan"; the printed page has "pal’i tamareinnan" (checked on the page image).
- The old rule pack limited this to "certain" compound nouns; the page states it for compound nouns in general.
- What counts as a compound noun: RULE_MOR_NOUN_COMPOUND_001 (§2.2.2.1 II, p. 8).

## Rule

For persons, Papiamento in some cases marks the distinction in the noun itself, with a suffix.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §2.4.1 "Distincion cu un elemento morfologico", p. 12: "Ora ta trata di persona ta haci den algun caso un distincion morfologico".

## Correct

- maestro - maestra
- suegro - suegra
- director - directora
- profesor - profesora
- actor - actriz
- emperador - emperatriz/emperadora

## Notes

- The page says "den algun caso" (in some cases) and gives a list, not a productive rule, so a checker should not build masculine or feminine forms that are not attested.
- General statement: RULE_MOR_GENDER_001. Gender with a different or added word: RULE_MOR_GENDER_003.

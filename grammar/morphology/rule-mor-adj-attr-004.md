## Rule

Some adjectives have a different meaning depending on whether they stand after or before the noun.

## Source

Manual di Gramatica di Papiamento: Morfologia (2010), §3.3.1.3 "Adhetivo patras of dilanti di sustantivo cu nificacion diferente", p. 18: "ora e adhetivo tin nificacion diferente, segun e posicion sintactico di e adhetivo".

## Correct

- E señora pober no tin placa pa paga bus (situacion financiero).
- E pober señora ta sufri hopi (duele).
- Doctor Moises Da Costa Gomes no tabata un homber grandi (estatura).
- Doctor Moises Da Costa Gomes tabata un gran homber (status).
- E ta un hende malo (salud).
- E ta un mal hende (caracter).

## Notes

- In these examples grandi and malo take the forms gran and mal before the noun.
- Because some adjectives change meaning with their position, a checker should not move an adjective from one side of the noun to the other. Default position: RULE_MOR_ADJ_ATTR_001.

## Rule

The verb tin has the irregular gerund teniendo.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.2.2 Gerundio iregular, p. 48: "tin – teniendo".

## Correct

- tin – teniendo

## Notes

- The page prints no wrong form. The wrong form tinindo → teniendo is in lexicon/barbarismos (bar-0038) and in _meta/invariants.md (Aruban irregular gerunds).
- The AB 2018 word list has teniendo (word list p. 66).
- Part of the full list in RULE_MOR_GERUND_004; kept as its own file because bar-0038 links to this id.

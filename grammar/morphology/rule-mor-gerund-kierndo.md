## Rule

The verb kier has the irregular gerund keriendo.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.2.2 Gerundio iregular, p. 48: "kier – keriendo".

## Correct

- kier – keriendo

## Notes

- The page prints no wrong form. The wrong form kierndo → keriendo is in lexicon/barbarismos (bar-0039) and in _meta/invariants.md (Aruban irregular gerunds).
- The AB 2018 word list has keriendo (word list p. 39).
- kier is a modal and takes no ta (_meta/invariants.md); this file only gives its gerund form, as the page does.
- Part of the full list in RULE_MOR_GERUND_004; kept as its own file because bar-0039 links to this id.

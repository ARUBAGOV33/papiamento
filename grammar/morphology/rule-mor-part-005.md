## Rule

If a verb has more than two syllables and ends in e, the participle changes the final e to i. The book notes that some people keep the infinitive form as the participle, and recommends applying this rule.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.3.1 Formacion, rule 4 and Nota, p. 49: "Ta recomenda pa haci uzo corecto di e regla aki."

## Correct

- [man-te-né] → [man-te-ní] E señora, a pesar di su edad, ta bon manteni.
- [o-fen-dé] → [o-fen-dí] E polis a wordo ofendi pa un choller.

## Incorrect

- mantene → manteni
- ofende → ofendi
- defende → defendi

## Notes

- The incorrect forms are the ones the Nota prints ("mantene en bes di manteni"), used as participles; mantene, ofende and defende are correct as infinitives.
- Severity is warning because the book words this as a recommendation and acknowledges the other usage. It also calls the rule "uzo corecto", so a reviewer may prefer error.
- The accent marks are only in the bracketed pronunciation notation (_meta/invariants.md: no accent marks).
- Related: RULE_MOR_PART_003 (two-syllable verbs ending in e).

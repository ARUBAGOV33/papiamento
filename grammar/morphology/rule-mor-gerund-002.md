## Rule

If the verb ends in e or i, that final vowel is dropped and iendo is added.

## Source

Morfologia, §6.6.2.1 Formacion, rule 2, pp. 47–48: "Si e verbo ta caba cu vocal e of vocal i, ta laga e vocal e of e vocal i cay afo y ta pone iendo acerca".

## Correct

- come → com + iendo → comiendo
- bolbe → bolb + iendo → bolbiendo
- bisti → bist + iendo → bistiendo
- skirbi → skirb + iendo → skirbiendo

## Exceptions

- When the i of iendo comes between two vowels it becomes y (Nota, p. 48); see RULE_MOR_GERUND_003.
- The irregular gerunds in §6.6.2.2 (p. 48); see RULE_MOR_GERUND_004.

## Notes

- Like rule 1, this applies "Generalmente" to verbs that come from Spanish (p. 47). It describes formation and flags nothing by itself.
- For drumi this rule gives drumiendo, the form the maintainer ruled (err-0009), although Morfologia lists durmiendo as irregular. See RULE_MOR_GERUND_004.

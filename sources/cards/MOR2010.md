## What the project uses it for

- Morphology rules in `grammar/morphology/`: word categories (ch. 1–11: sustantivo, adhetivo, articulo, adverbio, verbo, pronomber, conhuncion, numeral, preposicion, interheccion).
- Plural with -nan, gender, derived and compound words, degrees of comparison, verb forms and serial verbs; closed word classes in `lexicon/closed-classes/`; gerunds and participles (§6.6) in `lexicon/verb-forms/`.

## Known issues

- Scanned library copy (Biblioteca Nacional Aruba barcode on the cover). The private OCR text `MORFOLOGIA.txt` is noisy, e.g. symbols instead of page numbers in the contents. Check the page image.
- The colophon prints "Elvia Solognier- Croes" with a space; we write Solognier-Croes.
- Maintainer rulings that go against this book are logged in `sources/errata.tsv`: the p. 48 gerunds (err-0009 to err-0011), lo a on pp. 42, 44 and 57 (err-0019 to err-0021). err-0023 (ta gusta on p. 39) was decided for the book on 2026-10-19: gusta is used with or without ta.

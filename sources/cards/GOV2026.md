## What the project uses it for

- `lexicon/calendar/holidays.tsv`: which days are Aruba's public holidays, with their Papiamento names (from the Papiamento page), English names (English page) and Dutch names (Dutch page).
- Capitalisation follows AB 2018 §9.3 i (gazette p. 31): names of holidays take a capital letter. The page's own yearly tables do the same; its short list at the top writes some names in lowercase.

## Known issues

- The page names some holidays in more than one way across the years, e.g. "aña di Rey" and "Dia di Rey", "Dia di Obrero" and "Dia di Labor/Dia di Obrero", and "Dialuna despues di Carnaval Grandi" until 2022. The other forms are given in each row's notes.
- It calls Ascension Day "Dia di Asuncion".
- There is no Spanish page, so the `es` column is empty.
- The yearly dates for 2024-2026 are in linked PDFs, which were not downloaded. The table gives the fixed date or the rule ("movable"), not a date per year.

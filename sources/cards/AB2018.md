## What the project uses it for

- The official word list (annex "Anexo: Vocabulario di Papiamento"): every headword in `lexicon/words/`. The gerunds and participles it prints as headwords are also in `lexicon/verb-forms/`, linked to their base verb.
- The spelling rules of the annex "Ortografia di Papiamento", §1–12: alphabet, vowels, diphthongs, triphthongs, consonants, syllables, prefixes, punctuation, capitals, apostrophe, writing together or apart (§11.8 numerals), loanwords (§12).
- Art. 1 sets the spelling; art. 2 repeals AB 1990 no. GT 60; art. 3 says it may also be cited as Ordenansa Nacional ortografia di Papiamento.

## Known issues

- The 2009 Vocabulario (VOC2009) and Papiamento.aw's list match the law's word list entry for entry except for a few changes: the law adds encabeza/encabezando as variants and prints Bongo, Cinematografo, Computarisando and Computerisando with a capital (see `sources/errata.tsv`). Word-list pages are shifted by one against VOC2009 (law page 1 = VOC2009 page 2).

- §11.8, p. 38 prints "23 > bintiseis". This is a typo: 23 is bintitres, bintiseis is 26. Logged as `err-0001` in [`sources/errata.tsv`](../errata.tsv). The same typo is in ORT2009 p. 38.
- Page numbers come from the text extraction, where numbering restarts: the law is pp. 1–4, the orthography annex pp. 3–45, the word list pp. 1–72. When citing a page, also give the section or say "word list". Word rows in `lexicon/words/` cite the word-list page.

## What the project uses it for

- The spelling rules §1–12 that the AB 2018 annex enacts (same section numbering), for `grammar/orthography/`. Use AB2018 as the primary citation once its PDF is in hand.
- History of the official orthography (Introduccion, pp. 1–2).

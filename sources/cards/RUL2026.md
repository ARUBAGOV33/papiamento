## What the project uses it for

A source to cite when a rule rests on a decision by the maintainer or the review board rather than on a printed page. Each ruling is recorded where it can be checked: `_meta/known-facts.tsv` (checked automatically on every change), `_meta/invariants.md` and `sources/errata.tsv`.

## Known issues

Cite a printed source whenever one exists. Use RUL2026 only for rules no book or law states, and say in the rule's notes which ruling it rests on.

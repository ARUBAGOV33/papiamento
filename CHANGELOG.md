## [Unreleased]

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- The release files (`tools/build_dist.py`, `release/dataset-card.md`) ship eight more tables as their own configurations: `closed_classes`, `names`, `verb_forms`, `prompts`, `eval_prompts` (a `test` split, held out; do not train on it), `audits`, `milestones` and `contributors`. `prompts` and `eval_prompts` are CC0, as the card now says. The parallel translations ship once the teacher pilot has filled and reviewed them. `features` (closed classes) and `related` (names) are lists in JSON.
- `lexicon/verb-forms/verb-forms.tsv`: 1,921 gerunds and participles, all `draft` (board card P1-28). 51 rows come from Morfologia (`MOR2010`) §6.6 and the participles of §3.3.1.3, §6.2.2.1 and §6.7, each with page and section: the formation examples, the irregular gerunds and participles, and the example sentences. 1,870 rows are gerunds (1,645) and participles (225) that the AB 2018 word list prints as headwords, each linked to its base verb by the formation rules of Morfologia §6.6.2.1 and §6.6.3.1. Only printed forms are listed; none is generated by rule. Morfologia's durmiendo, podiendo and prefiriendo (p. 48) have no rows, because of the rulings err-0009 to err-0011. Each row links the base verb and the form to their rows in `lexicon/words/` (`word_id`, `form_word_id`) and says whether the form is irregular. New schema `_meta/schema/verb-forms.schema.json` and ids `vfm-00001` onwards.
- `lexicon/names/names.tsv`: the 380 proper names of the AB 2018 word list (board card P1-13), each copied from its headword with the word-list page, an English gloss and status `draft`. `word_id` points to the headword in `lexicon/words/`, so nothing is copied twice. `kind` sorts them by the capitalisation rules of AB 2018 §9.3–9.4: demonyms (125), countries (87), religious names (23), peoples (21), regions (16), titles (16), places (13), zodiac signs (12), planets (12), holidays (9), institutions (9), continents (8), languages (7), islands (6), waters (5), persons (3) and other (8). `related` links 120 demonyms to their place (Arubano → Aruba). Left out: 11 capitalised headwords that are not names (AIDS, CD, DVD, LP, PC, L.S., NB, PS, T-shirt, T-weg, X-ray) and the four words the law capitalises by mistake (err-0003 to err-0006). New schema `_meta/schema/names.schema.json`, ids `nam-0001` onwards.
- The validator checks that a name's `word_id` is a headword in `lexicon/words/` whose lemma is the name (new schema key `word_refs`); `folder_refs` cells may hold several ids separated by `; `.
- The validator's `word_refs` check also covers the verb forms: `word_id` and `form_word_id` must each be the id of a `lexicon/words/` row whose lemma is the row's `lemma` or `form`.
- `lexicon/closed-classes/`: 181 closed-class words from Morfologia (`MOR2010`), each with the printed page and section, an English gloss and status `draft` (board card P1-12). Six files: articles (5), pronouns (70: personal, possessive, reflexive, reciprocal, demonstrative, relative, interrogative, indefinite), tense/aspect particles (6), dependent verbs (20: modal, passive and copulative), conjunctions (50) and prepositions (30: simple, compound and prepositional expressions). Forms are copied from the page images; slash and bracket variants (cua/cual, ora (cu)) go in `alt`. The book's compound particle lo a has no row, because the maintainer ruled that lo and a never combine (err-0019). New schema `_meta/schema/closed-classes.schema.json` with a fixed vocabulary for `features`, and ids `ccl-0001` onwards. The verb forms (gerunds and participles) follow in a separate change.
- `lexicon/calendar/holidays.tsv`: Aruba's 11 public holidays with their Papiamento, English and Dutch names, a fixed date or "movable", from the Government of Aruba's holidays page (new source `GOV2026`, cited only). Capitalised as AB 2018 §9.3 i requires. Spanish stays empty: there is no official Spanish page.
- Parallel sentences, decided by the maintainer on 2026-10-18 (contribution tool design, board card P2-09): empty tables for English and Spanish prompts and their Aruban Papiamento translations in `corpus/parallel/`, with a 15% hash-based hold-out in `eval/parallel/`; schemas `_meta/schema/parallel-prompts.schema.json` and `parallel-pairs.schema.json`. Both are CC0 1.0 (exception in LICENSE-DATA), so they can go to Common Voice. They cite no source page: `translated_by` (or `author`) and `batch` are their provenance.
- `_meta/contributors.tsv`: everyone the data credits, by handle, credit name and role, with no contact details. Contributors no longer need a GitHub account; review-board members and maintainers still do.
- `tools/contribute/supabase/`: the contribution tool's database for the Supabase project `papiamento-contribute`. A migration with access rules (row-level security) enforces blind double answers, no self-rating, no deciding on your own item, and the board and maintainer roles; email addresses stay in Supabase's auth only. It comes with a rollback-only test of every rule (`rls-test.sql`), the sign-in email template, a paste-once guide, and the **Contribution security check** workflow, which proves the public key alone reads and writes nothing. It replaces the sketch in `tools/contribute/proposal/`.
- Prompts batch 1: 300 everyday English and Spanish sentences written for the project (machine-drafted, `author = machine`, `draft` until a maintainer checks them), on home, family, school, shopping, health, nature, transport, work, food, public services, time, leisure and everyday talk. 255 are in `corpus/parallel/prompts.tsv` and 45 (15%) are held out in `eval/parallel/prompts.tsv`. `tools/add_prompts.py` adds a batch, assigns the hold-out and refuses prompts that break the rules or repeat.
- `tools/word_frequency.py`: ranks headwords by how often they occur in the shareable source PDFs, blended with contributed sentences, to order the words to translate first.
- The validator checks that every name in `translated_by`, `author`, `checked_by`, `reviewed_by`, `ruled_by` and `auditor` is `machine` or a handle in `_meta/contributors.tsv` (new schema key `contributor_refs`), and that a parallel pair's prompt is in the same folder, so held-out prompts never mix with training data (`folder_refs`).
- `tools/apply_contributions.py` and the **Contribution sync** workflow: weekly, or by hand, they bring the review board's decisions from the contribution tool into `lexicon/words/`, `corpus/parallel/` or `eval/parallel/`, and `_meta/contributors.tsv`, and open one pull request through the papiamento-sync GitHub App, so the gauntlet runs on it. Rows stay `draft` unless a board member with a GitHub username decided them. Words already reviewed, sentences with spelling the validator refuses, and handle clashes are skipped and listed. Dry-run mode and tests on fixture data.
- `_meta/audits.tsv`, a log of random audits of each table against the source PDFs, with its schema, a template (`_templates/audit-row.tsv`) and conventions. It holds the six audits of the 2026-10 bug hunt (done by AI checker agents): 2 wrong machine translations out of 60, and no errors in samples of words (200), numbers (30), Curaçao-to-Aruba pairs (40), loanwords (40) and grammar files (20). The README progress table has a new "Measured error rate" column: the latest audit of each table with a 95% Wilson interval, or "not audited yet". The chart has a new Quality section. The validator checks that an audit's `errors` is never greater than its `sample_size`.
- `_meta/milestones.tsv`: the project phases P0 to P4 and where each stands, shown on one line above the README progress chart.
- `sources/errata.tsv`: err-0019 to err-0022, where Morfologia (pp. 42, 44, 57) and Sintaxis (p. 119) use lo a, against the ruling that lo and a never combine. The maintainer kept the ruling (2026-10-17). err-0023: Morfologia p. 39 and Sintaxis p. 31 allow ta with gusta, against the modal ruling; on 2026-10-19 the maintainer ruled for the books.
- `tools/contribute/`: a clickable prototype of the contribution tool (board card P2-09) and its design, `DESIGN.md`, with the maintainer's decisions of 2026-10-18 and the account setup checklist. Teachers and linguists check machine translations next to the printed AB 2018 page, translate words, and write everyday sentences in Aruban Papiamento with live checks from `rules/`, `lexicon/barbarismos/` and `lexicon/variants/`. It runs from a local file and stores answers only in the browser. Its Papiamento interface text is a draft for teacher review.
- The contribution tool's live site, published by the workflow "Contribution site" with GitHub Pages at https://arubagov33.github.io/papiamento/. When `config.js` says `live`, `tools/contribute/app.js` signs teachers in with an emailed code and uses the tool's database through supabase-js 2.117.2, which the deploy downloads and checks against its npm checksum. `tools/contribute/build_site.py` builds the site: every word to check, every untranslated word that occurs in the repo's texts (ordered by `tools/word_frequency.py`) and every prompt. It takes the Supabase URL and publishable key from the repository variables and refuses a secret key. The demo (`index.html` opened from the folder) works as before, and the validator skips the build folder `_site/`.
- The contribution tool has a lighter, more professional look: light by default, with Account → Appearance (Light, Dark, Match device); one accent colour, the Inter font self-hosted (SIL Open Font License, Latin and Latin Extended subsets, 133 KB) on one type scale, calmer cards, a tidier sidebar and a plain wordmark instead of the logo mark, with WCAG AA contrast in both themes. Fixed: a focus ring drawn around the page heading on every screen, and a board member's initials that slipped out of their avatar circle on a laptop (a class clash with the review board's page grid).

### Changed

- Project status: the table and chart count the verb forms, and `_meta/audits.tsv` can log an audit of the table `verb-forms`. In `_meta/milestones.tsv`, P1 no longer lists the verb forms as left to do, only their review.
- Project status: the table and chart count the names, and the "Still empty" line is gone: every table has rows. `_meta/audits.tsv` can log an audit of the table `names`.
- RULE_GRA_MODAL_TA_001 also flags ta before tin or tini after a pronoun (mi ta tin → mi tin): Morfologia §6.2.3.1 (p. 39) and Sintaxis §2.2.4.1 I (p. 29) both say tin(i) never takes ta. Added to `_meta/invariants.md`; the contribution tool's copy of the checks is rebuilt.
- Project status: the table and chart count the closed word classes, and "Still empty" now lists only names. `_meta/audits.tsv` can log an audit of the table `closed-classes`.
- Project status: the table and chart show the calendar with public holidays and the parallel sentences (prompts, held-out prompts, translations and reviews), so the picture covers the contribution tool's work.
- The contribution tool is live at https://arubagov33.github.io/papiamento/ (2026-10-19): the maintainer ran the database setup, and the access-rule test and the outside security check passed. README Project status and `_meta/milestones.tsv` say so.
- Version 0.1.0 is published on Hugging Face (https://huggingface.co/datasets/ARUBAGOV33/papiamento); the README links it.
- Version 0.1.0 is archived on Zenodo: README (badge, Project status, How to cite), `CITATION.cff` and the dataset card give the DOIs, 10.5281/zenodo.23427109 for 0.1.0 and 10.5281/zenodo.23427108 for all versions.
- Every rule that rested on a maintainer ruling was checked against the source pages, and the maintainer decided each one (2026-10-17):
  - Locative en becomes den (a closed or limited place) or na (a place in general), depending on the meaning (Preposicion y Interheccion pp. 6, 15). Barbarismo bar-0007 now gives `den; na`, and RULE_PRE_EN_001 flags en cas without an automatic fix.
  - RULE_MOR_GERUND_PREFIRIENDO no longer flags prefarando, a form from the old rule pack that no page prints.
  - `_meta/invariants.md` now follows the law on two spelling points. The -sion nouns of AB 2018 §5 (pp. 17–18) are an exception to -cion, and rooi, trei and fei (§3.2, p. 8) are exceptions to word-final y. The example hoy, which is not in the AB 2018 word list, is replaced.
  - The collective-noun invariant cites Stilistica (PDF p. 4) instead of a bare ruling, with the page's own example.
  - Barbarismo bar-0009 (del) now points to RULE_PRE_DE_001.
- The validator closes gaps that let wrong or missing data pass with CI green (found in the 2026-10 bug hunt; the maintainer chose each policy):
  - A registry table whose glob matches no file is an error, unless the registry marks it `"optional": true`. So is a missing `rules/rules.jsonl`, and `tools/test_rules.mjs` now fails without it. `build_dist.py` refuses to build a release in which a table, the grammar rules or the machine rules have no rows, and `build_pages.py` reads its table globs from the registry.
  - A machine rule may not flag any `## Correct` example, including those in its own grammar file (error). A rule lists deliberate exceptions in `allow_correct_hits`.
  - A known fact whose `where` matches no row is a warning. A fact on one row (`id=...`) is an error when that row's cell is emptied or the row is deprecated. The report lists the ids of pending facts.
  - `barbarismos.rule_id` must be the id of a grammar file, unless it is a row-level `RULE_LEX_*` id (warning). RULE_PRN_USTED_001 (bar-0005, bar-0006) is reported until it gets a grammar file or a `RULE_LEX_` id.
  - Rows with no source get an "untraced" warning. A PDF outside `sources/pdf/`, a hidden or line-breaking character in a cell (zero-width, U+2028, no-break space, control characters), and an unknown schema, constraint or `x-papiamento` key are errors.
- The Gauntlet, Refresh progress pages and Release workflows use actions/checkout v7.0.1, actions/setup-python v7.0.0 and actions/upload-artifact v7.0.2, the versions the contribution workflows already use. The old v4/v5 versions targeted the deprecated Node.js 20 (board card P0-15).
- `lexicon/loanwords/`: 18 of the 21 loanwords with origin `other` now have a sourced origin (maintainer decision of 2026-10-20, board card P1-27). AB 2018 §12.2 lists Dutch, English and French loans together without saying which is which, so each origin comes from a dictionary (CNRTL, WNT, Etymologiebank, Merriam-Webster) or, for brand names, the brand owner, cited in the row's notes with the meaning. 13 are `en` (spring, ring, ijscream, piccalilly and the US brands clorox, jello, jockey, keds, kleenex, kodak, kotex, osterizer, Q-tips), 3 `fr` (caret, comer < commère, comper < compère), 1 `nl` (wak < waken) and 1 `es` (choller < Latin-American Spanish cholo, although AB lists it with the Dutch, English and French loans). rom, piks and coecoei stay `other`; their notes say what was checked. The 18 origins are known facts fact-0043 to fact-0060, and the schema says what `other` now means. The rows stay `draft`.

### Fixed

- The contribution tool's copy of the checks (`tools/contribute/data/checks.js`) is rebuilt with the rulings of PR #30: the "Contribution site" deploy had stopped because the copy still had the old modal rule. The Gauntlet now runs `tools/contribute/test_checker.mjs`, so a pull request that changes the rules without rebuilding the copy fails before the merge.
- Rows found wrong in the 2026-10 bug hunt (checked against the page images): the false-friend sense "to slap" removed from `slap` (pap-013995), "opposition" removed from `opositor` (pap-010715), the copied language-name translation removed from lowercase `papiamento` (pap-010959); notes added to `num-0101` (shen, shento and cien before y, AB 2018 §11.8) and `var-0272` (muchu meaning "a lot" becomes hopi). The AB 2018 source card and `sources/sources.tsv` now say the +2 page offset holds only for the orthography annex.
- `sources/errata.tsv`: err-0017 (Sintaxis p. 36 "masha feros" vs AB 2018 feroz) and err-0018 (Morfologia p. 25 "poco poco" vs AB 2018 pocopoco). The maintainer decided both on 2026-10-19: AB 2018 is right and the books have typos. Both are in `_meta/invariants.md`.
- Barbarismos bar-0005 (usted) and bar-0006 (ustedes) point to the row-level id RULE_LEX_USTED_001 instead of RULE_PRN_USTED_001, an old Skirbi id with no grammar file, so the validator no longer warns about them.
- RULE_ORT_S_Z_001 keeps flagging lus: it is not a Papiamento word (luz is the noun, lusa the verb; maintainer decision on 2026-10-19). Its tests now check that lusa is not flagged.
- gusta is used with or without ta (maintainer ruling of 2026-10-19, err-0023): "E mucha ta gusta core" and "E mucha gusta core" are both correct. The modal invariant no longer lists gusta; RULE_GRA_MODAL_TA_001 keeps leaving it out and now has the book's sentence, read with and without ta, as Correct examples and tests.
- conoce never takes ta (maintainer ruling of 2026-10-19, following Morfologia §6.2.3.1, p. 39): "Mi tata conoce nos bisiña". Sintaxis p. 31, which allows ta with conoce, is logged as err-0024. RULE_GRA_MODAL_TA_001 now also flags ta before conoce after a pronoun (mi ta conoce → mi conoce).
- The loanword schema says what origin `other` means: AB 2018 does not tell which language the word comes from (the 21 rows' notes say why).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-16

First public release. 16,218 words, 159 numbers, 19 calendar terms, 42 barbarismos, 372 Curaçao-to-Aruba pairs, 315 loanwords, 192 grammar rules and 49 machine rules. Every data row is `draft`: the review board has not reviewed any yet. The 16 errata are `reviewed` (decided by the maintainer).

### Added

- `lexicon/words/A`–`Z`: all 16,218 headwords of the AB 2018 no. 68 word list, with alternative forms, the law's own labels, part of speech where the law gives it, and the printed page. Every row is `draft`; 6 rows are `disputed` because the law appears to contain a typo (`sources/errata.tsv`, err-0003 to err-0008). Translations are empty except two confirmed by the maintainer.
- `sources/pdf/AB2018.pdf`: the official law, from the archive.org copy uploaded by Biblioteca Nacional Aruba.
- `tools/import_ab2018.py` and `tools/import_ab2018_overrides.tsv`: how the word list was rebuilt from the PDF, and every correction made during review.
- `tools/build_pages.py`: generated progress page in each letter folder; CI checks they are up to date.
- English, Dutch and Spanish drafts for the 3,000 most frequent words (2,924 rows filled; 91 left empty on purpose). Drafted by one model, checked cell by cell by a second, disagreements settled by a third; no dictionaries, websites or Glosbe. 170 rows the drafting model rated uncertain carry a note asking reviewers to check them first.
- `translated_by` column in word files: `machine` for machine drafts, otherwise GitHub usernames. The validator requires it whenever a translation is filled.
- Translation conventions in `_meta/conventions.md`.
- `lexicon/numbers/`: cardinals 0-99, 100, 1,000, 1,000,000 and 1,000,000,000 (`biyon`, ruled to mean 10^9) from the AB 2018 word list; ordinals, fractions and multiples from Morfologia §9.3; clock times and worked examples from AB 2018 §10.1.3 and §11.8. 159 rows with English, Dutch and Spanish.
- `lexicon/calendar/days.tsv` and `months.tsv`: the 7 days and 12 months, lowercase, from AB 2018 §9.6 and the word list. Public holidays wait for an official source.
- Number kind `multiple` in the numbers schema.
- All 33 known facts are now checked against data (none pending), including the new rulings never = `nunca` and billion = `biyon`.
- `lexicon/barbarismos/barbarismos.tsv`: 41 forms to avoid with the Aruban form to use: Spanish day and month names, familia, hay, todos, muchos, usted(es), en, de la/del/de los/de las, siete (maintainer rulings, with the correct form cited in AB 2018); tevreden and eigenlijk (Stilistica); four wrong gerund forms (Morfologia p. 48, AB 2018). Rows where the AB 2018 list itself contains the "wrong" form (todo, eigenlijk) are marked disputed with an errata entry.
- `lexicon/variants/curacao-aruba.tsv`: 372 Curaçao → Aruba spelling pairs. Each Curaçao form is printed on the cited page of a Curaçao-spelled source; each Aruban form is an AB 2018 headword. Checked pair by pair for false friends and non-standard spellings.
- `sources/errata.tsv`: err-0009 to err-0013. Morfologia p. 48 lists durmiendo, podiendo and prefiriendo as gerunds, against maintainer rulings (the AB 2018 list has drumiendo); Stilistica vs AB 2018 on eigenlijk; the ruling on todo vs AB 2018.
- `lexicon/loanwords/loanwords.tsv`: 315 loanwords from AB 2018 §12 (gazette pp. 41-44): Spanish words written in a set form, words keeping final z, -eza/-anza nouns; Dutch, English and French words kept in their original spelling or respelled, the two-option pairs, the sch table, special spellings and words derived from brand names; Italian, Latin and German words. Two checkers verified every row against the page images. The language of origin for §12.2 words is assigned by the editors, since the law groups Dutch, English and French.
- `sources/errata.tsv`: err-0014 to err-0016, where §12 and the law's own word list disagree. Decided by the maintainer: dagvaard, osterizer in lowercase, and both schuin and scuins (new loanword row).
- Loanword origins `it`, `la` and `de` in the schema.
- `grammar/`: 192 spelling and grammar rules rebuilt from the source PDFs (AB 2018 orthography annex, Morfologia, Preposicion y Interheccion, Sintaxis, Stilistica), one file per rule with source and printed page. Papiamento examples are quoted verbatim from the page; none were written by a machine. Eight writer agents and eight independent checkers verified every rule against the page image (33 corrections). Of the 222 old rule-pack rules, 30 are not files: duplicates merged into another rule, or statements no page supports (listed in `grammar/README.md`).
- Validator gate for grammar rule files (front matter, source and page, file name, required section), with tests.
- Source `RUL2026` for rules that rest on a maintainer ruling rather than a printed page.
- `rules/rules.jsonl`: 49 machine-readable checker rules (31 orthography, 9 morphology, 4 prepositions, 3 from maintainer rulings, 1 syntax, 1 style), 30 of them with an automatic fix. Every test sentence is printed in the linked grammar file or is a documented mechanical change of a printed example. Four writer agents and four independent checkers built them; the checkers judged precision on about 10,000 real Aruban sentences (no false alarms in their samples apart from foreign-language words in the no-accent rule). 31 flaggable grammar rules have no pattern because a regex cannot check them reliably; `rules/README.md` lists them with the reason.
- Validator gate G5 tests every machine rule against its grammar file; `tools/test_rules.mjs` runs the same tests in JavaScript (the Skirbi checker's engine) in CI. Patterns may not use `\b` or `\w`, which misfire next to ñ in JavaScript.
- `tools/build_dist.py`: builds the release files into `dist/` (never committed): one JSON Lines file per table, the grammar rules as JSON Lines, `rule_pack.json` for a writing checker (machine rules, barbarismos and Curaçao-to-Aruba pairs), the Hugging Face dataset card from `release/dataset-card.md`, a manifest with row counts and SHA-256 checksums, and release notes with the rows per table and status. CI checks that the build works.
- README banner, badges and a progress chart. The chart (`.github/assets/progress-*.svg`) and the table under it are rebuilt from the data by `tools/build_pages.py`, and a workflow refreshes them on `main` after every merge.
- "How this dataset was made" in the README, the Zenodo record and the dataset card: which parts were made with AI models, how they were checked, and why AI models are not listed as authors.
- Partner credit for Vadria Innovation Labs in the README, the Zenodo record and the dataset card, and a Partners section in `GOVERNANCE.md`.
- Release setup: `.zenodo.json` so that Zenodo archives each GitHub release with a DOI, and a release workflow that attaches the release files to the GitHub release and publishes them to Hugging Face. `RELEASING.md` gives the steps.
- Repository skeleton (version 0).
- `_meta/`: conventions for every data file, table schemas and the schema registry, Aruban spelling and grammar invariants, known facts checked on every change, and the allowlist of accented loanwords.
- `_templates/`: empty tables with the correct header row.
- Folder layout for `lexicon/`, `grammar/`, `rules/`, `corpus/` and `eval/`.
- `sources/`: source registry, source cards, errata table and permission records.
- `tools/validate.py`: the validator ("the gauntlet"), with gates G0 (source trace), G1 (format), G2 (schema), G3 (orthography), G4 (integrity) and G5 (known facts), and a GitHub Actions workflow that runs it on every pull request.
- `CLAUDE.md` and `AGENTS.md`: routing notes for AI agents.
- Community files: README, contributing guide, governance, code of conduct, citation metadata, issue forms, pull request template and code owners.
- Licences: Apache-2.0 for code (`LICENSE`) and CC BY 4.0 for data and documentation (`LICENSE-DATA`).

### Changed

- Maintainer decisions on all open errata (2026-10-16); err-0003 to err-0013 are now `reviewed`:
  - Six typos in the AB 2018 word list are corrected in the data (`bongo`, `cinematografo`, `computarisando`, `computerisando`; alternatives `coregimento` and `lisimbein`). Each row notes what the law prints, and a known fact guards each correction.
  - `drumiendo` confirmed over Morfologia's `durmiendo` (new barbarismo row); `por` and `prefera` have no gerund, against Morfologia p. 48.
  - `eigenlijk` is a valid word but should be avoided in careful writing; `todo` only in fixed expressions such as `en todo caso`, otherwise `tur`.
  - `hamas` also means "never".
- Removed the machine translation of `skit` ("sketch"): the law lists it among words from Dutch sch-.
- Known facts now wait while the checked cell is empty, since an empty cell means "not known yet".
